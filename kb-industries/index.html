AI operators that find OEM accounts, quote OCTG threading and precision CNC jobs, plan 5-axis machining, inspect UHV chambers, and manage customer inventory programs
Independent concept  ·  illustrative
Each AI operator is a commercial or manufacturing workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track captures the work: it finds oil & gas operators, semiconductor equipment OEMs and aerospace primes that need precision machining, OCTG threading or UHV chamber fabrication, qualifies the job, quotes the work, and tracks competitive positioning. The other track delivers the product: it plans CNC programs, sets up 5-axis operations, runs in-process inspection, performs clean-room assembly, leak-tests UHV chambers, and manages customer inventory. Point one at an RFQ, a drawing package or a threading spec and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.

Stand up a new AI operator in three steps: pick the workflow, assign each task to an agent, then name it and set the cadence. It joins the workforce.
Independent concept prototype prepared by Innvonix Tech Solutions for a conversation with K&B Industries. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or built on K&B systems or data. All operators and outputs are illustrative simulations; RFQs, machining parameters, thread specs, leak-test results and pricing are placeholders. A human machinist, engineer or quality manager reviews and approves every CNC program, first article, leak test and quote. This is not a production system and produces no deliverable for a real job.
