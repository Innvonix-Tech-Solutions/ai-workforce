Each AI operator is an Applied Technical Services (ATS) ISO/IEC 17025 materials testing, Phased Array NDT inspection, Root Cause Failure Analysis (RCFA), or NIST-traceable calibration workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track wins the work: it finds aerospace primes (Lockheed Martin, Boeing, Pratt & Whitney), power utilities (Southern Company, NextEra), and pipeline operators seeking Nadcap-accredited metallurgy testing and Phased Array NDT, quotes turn-key 500-specimen test suites, responds to national utility master inspection tenders, and tracks TIC market intelligence. The other track delivers engineering testing, lab analytics and operations: it qualifies nickel superalloy turbine blades under ISO/IEC 17025, ASTM E3/E8, & Nadcap standards (SEM-EDS fractography identifying high-cycle fatigue, gamma prime coarsening, and zero micro-void defects), optimizes automated ICP-OES chemical spectroscopy calibrations (R² >0.9999 linearity across 32 elemental channels, NIST SRM 1244 Inconel 718 verification), simulates MIL-STD-810 environmental walk-in test chamber 3D CFD thermal shock & airflow dynamics (-70°C to +180°C @ 15°C/min, ±1.0°C uniformity), and manages ASTM/ASME/ISO standard ECOs & ATS LIMS test method revisions. Point one at an aerospace test RFP, an SEM micrograph file, or an ATS LIMS sample telemetry feed and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a VP of Quality Engineering, Chief Metallurgist, or Laboratory Director to approve.
