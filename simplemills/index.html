Simple Mills · AI Commercial & Supply Chain Workforce
AI operators that build retailer line reviews, run shopper marketing, plan supply, and protect quality and clean-label claims
Independent concept  ·  illustrative
Each AI operator is a commercial or supply-chain workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track grows the business: it finds retail and channel opportunities, builds the retailer line-review and category story from POS data, runs shopper marketing and promotions, and watches the better-for-you set. The other track keeps shelves full and clean: it plans demand and supply, sources almond flour and clean-label ingredients, fulfills retailer orders on time and in full, and protects the gluten-free, Non-GMO and clean-label claims. Point one at a retailer, a promo or an order and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.

Stand up a new AI operator in three steps: pick the workflow, assign each task to an agent, then name it and set the cadence. It joins the workforce.
Independent concept prototype prepared by Innvonix Tech Solutions for a conversation with Simple Mills. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or built on Simple Mills or Flowers Foods systems or data. All operators and outputs are illustrative simulations; retailers, POS figures, prices, ingredients, lead times and inventory are placeholders. A human category manager, planner or quality lead reviews and approves every line review, promo, plan, shipment and claim.
