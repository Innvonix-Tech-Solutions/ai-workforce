AI operators that capture Tier-1 EV & aerospace OEMs, design Battery Interconnect Systems (BIS), engineer continuous roll-laminated flex circuits, and certify ISO 26262 ASIL-D safety
Independent concept  ·  illustrative
Each AI operator is a high-current flexible printed circuit (FPC) commercial or manufacturing engineering workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track captures automotive & aerospace flex circuit business: it finds EV battery pack OEMs (Tesla, Rivian, General Motors), AI server data center builders, and aerospace contractors needing lightweight Battery Interconnect Systems (BIS) and flex-harnesses, quotes continuous roll production runs, structures harness weight-reduction trial proposals (-70% mass savings), and highlights CelLink's all-electric future vision. The other track delivers circuit engineering precision: it designs 800V EV battery interconnect flex-harnesses (300A continuous, 96 cell voltage/temperature channels), configures continuous roll-to-roll etch-free lamination, audits vehicle wiring mass reduction, and signs off ISO 26262 ASIL-D safety travelers. Point one at a harness drawing, a battery pack spec or a roll lamination log and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
