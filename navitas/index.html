Navitas Semiconductor Corporation (NASDAQ: NVTS, Torrance, California HQ with R&D in Dublin & Shanghai) is the pure-play leader in next-generation GaN and SiC power semiconductors under the banner “Electrify Our World™”. One track drives commercial sourcing & AI data center/EV OEM relationships: it captures AI Data Center PSU Manufacturers (Delta Electronics, Lite-On, Seasonic, Supermicro), EV Onboard Charger OEMs (Tesla, Hyundai/Kia, BYD, BorgWarner), and Solar & ESS Inverter Makers (Enphase, SolarEdge, Sungrow), quotes GaNFast™ IC & GeneSiC™ module volume orders, structures 22kW 800V EV onboard charger proposals, and highlights Navitas' GaNSense™ autonomous protection, 80+ Titanium PSU efficiency, and TSMC GaN wafer fab scale. The other track delivers power semiconductor engineering precision: it designs 3.2kW 80+ Titanium AI Data Center GaNFast™ PSUs (97.6% efficiency at 100 W/in³ power density), optimizes 22kW 800V EV onboard charger GeneSiC™ SiC MOSFET modules (99.1% efficiency), tests GaNSense™ autonomous short-circuit protection (< 30 ns fault-to-shutdown response), and audits Torrance CA wafer prober & high-voltage ATE yields (Cpk = 1.84). Point one at a power circuit schematic, an AI data center PSU spec or a wafer probe log and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
