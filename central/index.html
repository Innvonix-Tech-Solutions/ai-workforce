Each AI operator is a Central Garden & Pet Pennington Smart Seed, Kaytee pet nutrition, Sevin/Amdro lawn care, or DSD direct-to-store logistics workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track wins the work: it finds mass merchants (Walmart, Target, Home Depot, Lowe's), specialty pet chains (Petco, PetSmart), and independent garden centers seeking Pennington, Kaytee, Aqueon, and Nylabone product lines, quotes turn-key wholesale CPG contracts, responds to national retail master tenders, and tracks CPG lawn & pet retail market intelligence. The other track delivers CPG manufacturing and supply chain operations: it qualifies Pennington Smart Seed & Sevin insect control under EPA FIFRA, FDA, & USDA standards (seed germination rate 94.5% ± 0.5%, 30% less water, 99.2% beetle kill in 24 hrs, seed purity >99.8%), optimizes high-speed seed cleaning yields & checkweigher calibration (99.2% seed yield, ±0.05 oz bag accuracy), simulates barrier pouch heat sealing CFD thermal flow & pressure (185°C sealing at 120 bags/min, zero pinhole leak), and manages CPG formula & PCR plastic packaging ECO revisions. Point one at a retail merchant account, a seed purity lab sheet, or a packaging line G-code file and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a Senior Category Merchant, VP of Supply Chain, or Quality Assurance Director to approve.
