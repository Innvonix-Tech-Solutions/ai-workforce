Aeluma · AI Commercial & Product Engineering Workforce
AI operators that win defense contracts, design-in SWIR sensors, grow III-V epitaxy on silicon, qualify photodetectors, and scale photonic interconnects for AI infrastructure
Independent concept  ·  illustrative
Each AI operator is a commercial or engineering workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track captures the business: it finds defense programs, AI/HPC datacenter integrators and OEMs that need III-V-on-silicon photodetectors, quantum-dot lasers or SWIR sensors, qualifies the opportunity, responds to DARPA/Navy/NASA BAAs, and quotes NRE and wafer runs. The other track delivers the product: it designs the epitaxial structure, models the photodetector, runs the wafer growth, characterises the device, qualifies the sensor, and coordinates foundry transfer for volume production. Point one at a BAA, an OEM spec or a wafer run and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.

Stand up a new AI operator in three steps: pick the workflow, assign each task to an agent, then name it and set the cadence. It joins the workforce.
Independent concept prototype prepared by Innvonix Tech Solutions for a conversation with Aeluma, Inc. (ALMU). It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or built on Aeluma systems or data. All operators and outputs are illustrative simulations; contracts, wafer specs, device parameters, quantum efficiencies and pricing are placeholders. A human engineer, program manager or VP reviews and approves every epitaxial design, device characterisation, proposal and contract deliverable. This is not a production system and produces no deliverable for a real program.
