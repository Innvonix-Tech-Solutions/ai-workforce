Each AI operator is an optical PHY, LoRaWAN® wireless or circuit protection engineering workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track captures hyperscale data center & IoT silicon business: it finds AI cloud builders (AWS, Microsoft Azure, Google, Meta), optical transceiver makers (InnoLight, Coherent, Eoptolink, Cisco, Arista), and smart utility metering OEMs needing Tri-Edge™ PAM4 CDRs, FiberEdge® TIAs, and LoRa® chipsets, quotes silicon volume runs, structures Linear Pluggable Optics (LPO) evaluation proposals (-50% optical module power), and highlights Semtech's analog CDR latency advantage (<10ns). The other track delivers physical layer semiconductor precision: it configures 800G OSFP/QSFP-DD optical module PHYs (Tri-Edge™ PAM4 CDR + FiberEdge® 112 Gbaud TIA array), tunes LoRaWAN® LR1110 sub-GHz wireless links (-137 dBm sensitivity across 15 km), audits RClamp® ultra-low capacitance (<0.15pF) TVS ESD protection arrays, and signs off PerSe® smart proximity SAR sensor compliance travelers. Point one at an optical link spec, a LoRaWAN® gateway design or a TVS clamping log and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
