AI operators that find defense & aerospace accounts, quote bare die & packaging, qualify components, and manage supply chain
Independent concept  ·  illustrative
Each AI operator is a commercial or microelectronics engineering workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track wins defense & aerospace contracts: it finds defense primes, space OEMs, and medical device accounts needing high-rel microelectronics, quotes bare die, wafer processing, and hermetic packaging, responds to tenders, and tracks market intelligence. The other track delivers high-reliability engineering: it qualifies bare die and wafers under MIL-STD-883 standards, performs electrical screening & counterfeit detection, manages long-lead wafer inventory, and ensures AS9100 compliance. Point one at a defense customer, a wafer lot, or an engineering spec, and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a manager to approve.
