AI operators that find WLBI opportunities, qualify applications, design WaferPaks and DiePaks, develop recipes, and support the installed base
Independent concept  ·  illustrative
Each AI operator is a commercial or applications-engineering workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track wins the work: it finds wafer-level burn-in opportunities across silicon carbide, silicon photonics, AI processors and memory, qualifies a customer application from evaluation to production, quotes the FOX system and the WaferPak and DiePak consumables, and runs market intelligence. The other track delivers the work: it designs the device-specific WaferPak contactor or DiePak carrier, develops the burn-in and test recipe, supports the installed base, and plans consumables capacity. Point one at a wafer, an application or a system and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for an engineer to approve.
