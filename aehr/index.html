Each AI operator is a commercial or semiconductor WLBID engineering workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track wins the work: it finds SiC power fabs, GaN foundries, and silicon photonics transceiver manufacturers requiring full-wafer level burn-in, quotes FOX-XP™ multi-wafer systems and custom WaferPak™ contactor cards, responds to automotive EV zero-defect tenders, and tracks WLBID adoption. The other track delivers the test engineering and production: it runs 150-wafer/day FOX-XP™ SiC burn-in qualifications at 175°C, optimizes 20,000-pin WaferPak™ micro-probe alignment, generates 1200V HTGB/HTRB test vectors, and manages WaferPak™ pin wear ECOs. Point one at a semiconductor fab account, a 200mm SiC wafer map, or a WaferPak™ CAD file and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a test engineering manager to approve.
