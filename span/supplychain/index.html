SPAN · AI Commercial & Supply Chain Workforce
AI operators that grow the installer channel and run direct procurement, the 3-way match and consigned inventory
Innvonix concept  ·  illustrative
Each AI operator is a commercial or supply-chain workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track grows the business: it finds installers and homebuilders, quotes whole-home SPAN Panel and SPAN Drive projects, responds to utility VPP tenders, and watches the market. The other track runs the plant and the money: it manages direct procurement and the blanket PO, executes the 3-way match across the SPAN PO, supplier invoice and Mexico CM receiver, keeps consigned inventory in sync with the CM, qualifies the panel to UL, and plans production. Point one at an account, a PO or a shipment and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output. Your team approves every payment and every inventory change.
