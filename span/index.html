Independent concept  ·  illustrative
Each AI operator is a commercial electrification or smart panel manufacturing workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track wins the work: it finds solar & battery installers (Sunrun, SunPower), electrical contractors, and luxury homebuilders seeking SPAN Smart Panels to avoid 400A utility service upgrades, quotes turnkey home electrification packages, responds to municipal utility virtual power plant (VPP) demand response tenders, and tracks off-grid battery management adoption. The other track delivers the electromechanical engineering and manufacturing operations: it qualifies SPAN Panel 32-circuit solid-state relays and CT metering for UL 67 safety, optimizes SPAN Drive 48A EV charger dynamic load balancing and 200ms current throttling, simulates main busbar CFD thermal dissipation (junction temp <85°C), and manages PCB antenna ECO revisions. Point one at an installer partner account, a UL test report, or a panel CAD file and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a VP of Hardware Engineering or Director of Operations to approve.
