Each AI operator is a high-purity sputtering target qualification, 300mm fab PVD metallurgy, or thin-film materials sales workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track closes commercial PVD material contracts: it prospects global 300mm/200mm semiconductor fabs (TSMC, Intel, Samsung, GlobalFoundries), display OEMs (OLED/LCD) & solar PVD buyers, quotes high-purity sputtering targets (Cu 6N, Ta, Ti, Ti-W @ $14,500/target), responds to fab material RFPs with 99.9999% GDMS purity data, and tracks raw metal commodity markets (copper, tantalum, titanium). The other track delivers target metallurgy & PVD quality engineering: it qualifies diffusion-bonded backing plate assemblies, audits GDMS 6N impurity limits & grain orientation, diagnoses cleanroom target erosion & arcing faults, and plans high-purity metal ingot supply chains for Grove City, Ohio manufacturing. Point one at a fab PVD tender, a target drawing spec, or a spent target erosion issue, and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for metallurgy directors.
