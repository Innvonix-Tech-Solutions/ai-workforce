Each AI operator is an industrial motive power charging, Ascendrix high-frequency power stage synthesis, or ChargerConnect cloud telemetry workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. Lester Electrical (headquartered in Lincoln, Nebraska) has been a premier American designer and manufacturer of industrial battery chargers and power conversion equipment for over 60 years. Engineered for harsh industrial environments, Lester Electrical powers motive fleets across material handling, aerial work platforms, automated guided vehicles (AGVs), golf cars, utility vehicles, and heavy-duty utility substation stationary power systems. One track drives commercial sourcing & OEM fleet partnerships: it identifies Class III pallet jack and aerial lift OEMs transitioning to lithium-ion packs, sizes multi-voltage charging solutions, structures tiered volume CapEx pricing, and tracks global power electronics and battery chemistry innovations. The other track executes high-frequency switchmode power electronics engineering, multi-chemistry charging algorithms, vehicle telemetry handshakes, and life-safety compliance: it models interleaved PFC and LLC resonant topologies for the next-generation Ascendrix series, synthesizes CEC Title 20 compliant charge algorithms for LiFePO4 / lead-acid batteries, configures SAE J1939 CAN bus interlocks with drive-lockout protection, and monitors multi-site fleet battery health through ChargerConnect cloud analytics. Point one at an OEM vehicle specification, a battery chemistry datasheet, or a fleet charging log and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
