Each AI operator is a commercial sales or advanced PCB engineering workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track wins commercial contracts: it finds aerospace, defense, automotive, and data center server OEMs needing HDI PCBs and RF assemblies, quotes HDI, Rigid-Flex, and QTA quick-turn prototypes, responds to defense tenders, and tracks market intelligence. The other track delivers precision PCB manufacturing: it analyzes DFM layouts, designs high-density micro-via stackups, inspects IPC-Class 3 reliability, manages RF microelectronics SMT lines, and plans high-frequency PTFE laminate supply. Point one at a defense RFP, a Gerber PCB layout, or an RF module, and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a manager to approve.
