Each AI operator is an electronic test, environmental thermal control, or induction process technology workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track wins the work: it finds semiconductor ATE test labs, defense aerospace contractors (MIL-STD-883), and medical storage facilities seeking ThermoStream localized thermal testing and Acculogic Flying Probe systems, quotes turnkey test & thermal solutions, responds to automotive AEC-Q100 IC tenders, and tracks 3D IC test manipulator adoption. The other track delivers the test engineering and manufacturing operations: it qualifies ThermoStream ATS-545 rapid thermal transition (-55°C to +125°C in <15s), optimizes Ambrell EASYHEAT 10kW precision induction brazing, simulates North Sciences -86°C ultra-cold freezer cascade CFD thermal insulation, and manages ATE test head docking latch ECO revisions. Point one at a test customer account, a thermal transition plot report, or a manipulator CAD file and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a VP of Test Engineering or Director of Operations to approve.
