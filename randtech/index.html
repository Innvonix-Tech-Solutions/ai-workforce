Independent concept  ·  illustrative
Each AI operator is an electronic component sourcing, IDEA-STD-1010 counterfeit testing, excess inventory asset recovery, or AS9120B quality workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track wins the work: it finds automotive OEMs, aerospace defense contractors, and medical device manufacturers facing semiconductor shortages, quotes turn-key component sourcing and VMI buffer stock agreements, responds to global EMS master tenders, and tracks semiconductor grey market intelligence. The other track delivers testing laboratory and plant operations: it qualifies Automotive MCU IDEA-STD-1010 counterfeit avoidance & X-ray lead integrity (100% visual pass, X-ray 100%, Decap acid etch 100%, solderability >98%), optimizes automated IC tape-and-reel packaging & dry-pack sealing lines (15,000 ICs/hr output, >99.9% tape placement yield), simulates IC baking oven CFD thermal fluid kinetics (125°C ±1.0°C uniform desorption field), and manages component cross-reference spec ECO revisions. Point one at an OEM customer account, an IDEA inspection report, or a component packaging line run and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a VP of Global Quality or Director of Component Engineering to approve.
