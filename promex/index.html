Each AI operator is a microelectronics packaging, heterogeneous 2.5D/3D integration, Class 100/1000 medtech assembly, or silicon photonics workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track wins the work: it finds medtech device OEMs, optical transceiver developers, and defense contractors seeking System-in-Package (SiP) and Class 1000 cleanroom assembly, quotes turn-key NPI packaging agreements, responds to global master packaging tenders, and tracks microelectronics market intelligence. The other track delivers cleanroom manufacturing and plant operations: it qualifies Class 1000 Medtech SiP flip chip attach & fine-pitch wire bonding (coplanarity <3 µm, wire shear >15g, underfill void-free >99.8%), optimizes Class 1000 cleanroom SMT reflow & wafer dicing lines (35,000 CPH, kerf 25 µm ±1.0 µm), simulates SiP mold encapsulation & capillary underfill CFD fluid kinetics (100% void-free fill in <18 sec), and manages assembly spec ECO revisions. Point one at a medtech customer account, a wire bond test report, or a cleanroom SMT batch log and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a VP of Packaging Engineering or Director of Quality Assurance to approve.
