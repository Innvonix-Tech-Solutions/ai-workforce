Independent concept  ·  illustrative
Each AI operator is a utility-scale solar tracker commercial or structural engineering workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track wins commercial contracts: it prospects utility PV project permits (>50MW), quotes Voyager™ 1P & Pioneer™ 2P solar tracker hardware, responds to EPC RFPs with structural wind load models, and tracks solar tracker market trends. The other track delivers site engineering & operational support: it ingests site topography CAD maps, runs SUNPath® intelligent backtracking algorithms, audits wind tunnel stowing compliance (160 mph), diagnoses field drive motor telemetry, and plans steel torque tube supply chains. Point one at a solar developer RFP, a site CAD layout, or a tracker field diagnostic log, and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for engineering leadership sign-off.
