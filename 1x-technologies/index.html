Each AI operator is a commercial sales or humanoid robotics deployment workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track wins commercial contracts: it finds logistics operators, commercial security providers, facility management firms, and enterprise accounts needing humanoid robots, quotes NEO & EVE robot fleets, responds to tenders, and tracks embodied AI market intelligence. The other track delivers precision robotics operations: it maps site CAD layouts, trains neural network policies via teleoperation, audits ISO 13482 safety compliance, services joint actuators, and plans spare parts. Point one at an enterprise customer, a site CAD layout, or a robot telemetry log, and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a manager to approve.
