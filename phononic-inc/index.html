Phononic, Inc. (headquartered in Durham, North Carolina) is a global leader in solid-state semiconductor cooling, specializing in micro-thermoelectric coolers (TECs) and Peltier Thermal Control Systems™ (TCS™). Phononic provides chip-level active thermal management for AI data center optical transceivers (1.6T, 3.2T, CPO co-packaged optics, TOSA/TO-can, QSFP-DD), 5G/6G wireless infrastructure, and licensing cold-chain fulfillment. One track drives commercial sourcing & optical engine relationships: it captures Optical Transceiver Vendors (Coherent/Finisar, Lumentum, Innolight, Eoptolink, Cisco/Acacia), AI Hyperscalers (Microsoft, AWS, Google, Meta), and Silicon Photonics Vendors, quotes 1.6T Micro-TEC supply contracts (250,000 TEC modules/year), structures 3.2T CPO thermal control proposals, and highlights Phononic's high Coefficient of Performance (COP > 1.4), automated Durham NC manufacturing, and Telcordia GR-468 qualification. The other track delivers thermoelectric solid-state semiconductor cooling engineering precision: it designs high-heat-flux Micro-TEC junctions (Bi2Te3 semiconductor legs, ZT > 1.15, Qmax = 4.8W, ΔTmax = 72°C), stabilizes TOSA EML laser junction temperatures (50°C ± 0.1°C, wavelength drift < 0.05 nm at 85°C ambient, 58% energy savings), executes Telcordia GR-468-CORE reliability qualification (−40°C to +125°C thermal cycling, 1,500g mechanical shock), and audits Durham NC fab micro-assembly lines (TEC resistance Cpk = 1.94, automated probe takt time 8s/module). Point one at an optical laser thermal spec, a TEC resistance log or a Telcordia qualification trace and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
