AI operators that discover OEM robotics/medical partners, analyze DFM & BOMs, quote PCBA/box builds, and audit ISO 13485 quality
Concept Prototype  ·  illustrative
Each AI operator is a commercial or precision manufacturing workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track wins the business: it discovers OEM robotics and medical device partners, analyzes DFM (Design for Manufacturability), generates rapid PCBA and box build quotes, responds to tenders, and monitors supply chain lead times. The other track delivers the precision: it automates bill-of-materials (BOM) scrub and component sourcing, coordinates automated optical inspection (AOI) and flying probe test protocols, manages ISO 13485 / AS9100 quality compliance, and maintains real-time shop floor throughput. Point one at an engineering package, BOM, or RFP, and it runs the loop, returning a review-ready output for engineering and sales management approval.

Stand up a new AI operator in three steps: pick the workflow, assign each task to an agent, then name it and set the cadence. It joins the workforce.
Independent concept prototype prepared for a conversation with Cirtronics Corporation. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or built on Cirtronics systems or data. All operators and outputs are illustrative simulations; quotes, component lead times, DFM feedback, test coverage, and ISO audit points are placeholders. A senior manufacturing engineer, quality manager, or commercial director reviews and approves every quote, DFM package, test protocol, and compliance check. This simulation produces no physical manufacturing release.
