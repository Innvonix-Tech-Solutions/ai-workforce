Each AI operator is a high-power semiconductor or heavy traction/defense conversion workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. Powerex, Inc. (Youngwood, Pennsylvania manufacturing fab, established in 1986 as a joint venture co-owned by GE & Mitsubishi Electric) is a global leader in mission-critical power semiconductors. One track drives commercial sourcing & heavy industry OEM relationships: it captures Electric Locomotive OEMs (Wabtec, Alstom, Siemens Mobility), Renewable Grid Integrators (GE Vernova, Siemens Energy), and Defense Naval Contractors, quotes 3.3kV SiC MOSFET & HVIGBT volume orders, structures custom ITAR military power assembly proposals, and highlights Powerex's Youngwood PA fab scale, AS9100D certification, and CSTBT™ trench-gate technology lead. The other track delivers power module engineering & high-voltage testing precision: it designs 3.3kV Full-SiC MOSFET traction modules (65% lower switching loss), programs Pow-R-Blok™ isolated SCR/diode modules (2,500V dielectric isolation), executes 6.5kV surge & partial discharge testing (>5.1kV extinction voltage), and audits Youngwood PA fab die-attach & wirebond yields (>350g pull strength). Point one at a power module datasheet, a traction inverter RFQ or a high-pot surge log and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
