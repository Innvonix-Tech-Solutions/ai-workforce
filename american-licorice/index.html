Each AI operator is an American Licorice Company commercial retail sales, licorice continuous extrusion, Sour Punch acid sanding, SQF food safety, or dual-plant logistics workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track expands retail brand reach and co-manufacturing volume: it scouts top grocery banners (Walmart, Target, Kroger), warehouse club networks (Costco, Sam's Club), and national movie theater concessionaires (AMC, Regal, Cinemark), quotes 15M-lb annual turnkey licorice extrusion runs across La Porte, IN and Union City, CA facilities, structures club store multi-pack theater tub master tenders, and tracks non-chocolate candy competitive retail velocities against Twizzlers and Sour Patch Kids. The other track safeguards candy science, continuous extrusion operations, and TRUE Zero Waste Gold environmental compliance: it qualifies continuous extrusion lines with dual-drum citric/malic acid sugar sanding systems, monitors steam-jacketed batch cooker gelatinization and cooling tunnel moisture equilibrium (1,200 twists/minute at 15.5% finished moisture), simulates multi-tier cooling tunnel airflow dynamics with 3D CFD thermal modeling, and audits all-natural colorant reformulations (replacing Red 40 with radish and red beet extracts). Point one at a retail buyer solicitation, an SQF audit checklist, or La Porte extruder telemetry and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a VP of Sales, Director of Confectionery Operations, or VP of Quality to approve.
