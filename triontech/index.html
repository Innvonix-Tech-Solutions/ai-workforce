Each AI operator is a plasma etching, PECVD thin film deposition, or photoresist stripping workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track wins the work: it finds compound semiconductor fabs (GaAs, GaN, InP), university nanotechnology centers, and MEMS foundries seeking Phantom RIE ICP, Orion PECVD, and Apollo resist strippers, quotes turnkey plasma processing tools, responds to global university cluster tenders, and tracks cleanroom expansion. The other track delivers the plasma process engineering and fab operations: it qualifies Phantom RIE ICP GaN etch rates & anisotropic sidewall profiles (>88° angle @ 4,500 Å/min), optimizes Apollo plasma stripper photoresist ash rates (>3.0 µm/min with zero oxide damage), simulates vacuum loadlock pump-down CFD dynamics (atmosphere to 10^-3 Torr in <38 sec), and manages gas box manifold ECO revisions. Point one at a fab customer account, an ellipsometry test report, or a PLC recipe file and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a VP of R&D or Cleanroom Fab Director to approve.
