AI operators that find defense and space programs, source hi-rel die and components, upscreen to MIL-STD-883, package MCMs, and sustain the installed base
Independent concept  ·  illustrative
Each AI operator is a commercial or engineering workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track captures the work: it finds defense primes and space programs that need hi-rel microelectronics, qualifies the requirement against Micross capabilities, responds to RFQs and sole-source justifications, and tracks the competitive and obsolescence landscape. The other track delivers the product: it sources bare die and wafers, upscreens plastic parts to MIL-STD-883, builds hermetic MCM packages, runs lot acceptance testing, and sustains legacy programs against DMS obsolescence. Point one at a program, a part number or a sustainment case and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.

Independent concept prototype prepared by Innvonix Tech Solutions for a conversation with Micross Components. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or built on Micross systems or data. All operators and outputs are illustrative simulations; programs, part numbers, pricing, test results, packaging specs and lead times are placeholders. A human engineer, quality manager or program manager reviews and approves every die source, upscreen, package design, test report, quote and compliance check. This is not a production system and produces no deliverable for a real program.
