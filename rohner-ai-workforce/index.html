Rohner Finishing Systems (rohnerspraybooths.com) crafts precision industrial spray booths, batch cure ovens, automated powder lines, and large aerospace finishing enclosures in Vancouver, Washington. Each AI operator below is a custom finishing system workflow turned into an autonomous team of specialist agents. Track 1 drives pre-sale engineering & commercial quotation: it parses customer part envelopes, calculates crossdraft/downdraft airflow (CFM) & burner BTUs, designs PLC control interlocks, validates NFPA 33 fire codes, and compiles priced custom BOM proposals. Track 2 delivers fabrication, project delivery & field commissioning: it synthesizes multi-stage part pretreatment wash enclosures, produces submittal CAD packages, sequences shop fabrication, and oversees on-site airflow balancing and FAT commissioning handovers. Point an operator at a customer RFQ, an engineering specification, or an on-site airflow test, and it executes the entire loop.
