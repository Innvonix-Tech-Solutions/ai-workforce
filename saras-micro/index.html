Independent concept  ·  illustrative
Each AI operator is an AI/HPC semiconductor commercial or advanced power delivery engineering workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track captures chipmaker business: it finds GPU/NPU chipmakers, hyperscale data center architects, and CHIPS NAPMP advanced packaging consortiums needing STILE™ embedded power delivery, scopes custom substrate NRE, structures joint development agreements (JDAs), and highlights Vertical Power Delivery (VPD) advantages. The other track delivers power integrity engineering: it embeds STILE™ capacitive tiles directly beneath compute die, executes 3D EM Power Delivery Network (PDN) impedance simulations ($Z(f) < 2\,\text{m}\Omega$), routes 2.5D/3D interposer micro-bumps, runs 700W TDP thermal FEA modeling, verifies Vdroop transient response (<15 mV), and releases substrate fabrication travelers. Point one at a PDN specification, a package layout or an S-parameter deck and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
