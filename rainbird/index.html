Each AI operator is a commercial or intelligent irrigation manufacturing workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track wins the work: it finds agricultural irrigation dealers, golf course superintendents, and municipal parks departments seeking CirrusPRO™ IC Systems and IQ4™ Smart Cloud Controllers, quotes turnkey golf & farm projects, responds to municipal smart water tenders, and tracks 2-wire central control adoption. The other track delivers the hydraulic engineering and manufacturing operations: it qualifies 5000 Series Rain Curtain™ rotor nozzles for spray Distribution Uniformity (DU >0.86), optimizes PGA solenoid valve ultrasonic welding and 200 PSI hydrostatic leak testing at Azusa CA, simulates subsurface pressure-compensating drip line CFD hydraulics, and manages controller firmware ECOs. Point one at an agricultural distributor account, a rotor catch-can lab report, or a valve CAD file and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for an irrigation engineer or Director of Manufacturing to approve.
