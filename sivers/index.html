Sivers Semiconductors · AI SIOP Workforce
AI operators that run the monthly demand, inventory and operations plan across Wireless and Photonics
Innvonix concept  ·  illustrative
Each AI operator is a SIOP workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track owns demand: it builds the consensus forecast from orders and design-wins, captures customer commitments, and watches the market across SATCOM, defense, fixed-wireless and AI-datacenter optics. The other owns supply: it reviews GlobalFoundries wafer allocation for the fabless Wireless side and the in-house III-V fab capacity for Photonics, balances inventory across WIP and finished goods, plans procurement, and runs the monthly SIOP reconciliation that turns the demand-supply gap into allocation scenarios and an executive pack. Point one at a month and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for your planners and exec team to approve.

A human planner and the executive team review and approve every plan, allocation and commitment; the operators prepare and recommend, they do not decide.
