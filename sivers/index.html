Integrated Sales, Inventory & Operations Planning (SIOP) AI platform unifying Wireless (5G/6G mmWave, SATCOM BFICs) & Photonics (InP CW lasers for AI optical interconnects)
Independent concept  ·  illustrative
Each AI operator is a Sales, Inventory, and Operations Planning (SIOP) semiconductor workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. Sivers Semiconductors integrates two world-class divisions—Sivers Wireless (5G/6G mmWave beamforming RFICs, SATCOM Ka-band chips, FWA) and Sivers Photonics (InP DFB lasers, CW lasers for AI co-packaged optics). One track drives SIOP commercial demand: it captures 5G mmWave telecom OEMs (Ericsson, Nokia, Samsung), SATCOM flat-panel terminal makers (ALL.SPACE, Hanwha), and AI data center interconnect developers, quotes volume RFIC/laser orders against real-time wafer fab capacity, structures multi-year SATCOM SIOP supply contracts, and provides competitive market intel. The other track executes SIOP operations and semiconductor engineering precision: it balances Glasgow InP fab wafer starts for 8-channel AI CW laser arrays (70mW/ch), designs 28/39 GHz 5G beamforming RFICs (EIRP > 42 dBm), optimizes die bank safety stock ($4.2M inventory buffer), and consolidates executive Integrated Business Planning (IBP) revenue & gross margin forecasts. Point one at a 5G RFIC order, an InP laser wafer lot or a SIOP demand forecast and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for executive sign-off.
