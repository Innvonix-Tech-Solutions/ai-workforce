Independent concept  ·  illustrative
Each AI operator is a high-voltage power semiconductor commercial or power conversion engineering workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track captures power electronics business: it finds consumer fast-charger brands (Anker, Belkin, Samsung), EV traction inverter OEMs (Tesla, BYD, Rivian), and AI data center power builders requiring PowiGaN™ ICs, InnoSwitch™4, and SCALE™ gate drivers, quotes high-volume IC tape-and-reel packages, structures SCALE™ gate driver board proposals, and highlights Power Integrations eco-design leadership. The other track delivers power semiconductor precision: it configures 100W USB-PD 3.1 PowiGaN™ flyback converters ($95.2\%$ efficiency), designs TOPSwitch™-GaN 440W non-resonant converters, simulates SCALE EV dual-channel gate driver double-pulse tests, audits EcoSmart™ zero-standby power consumption (<5mW), and signs off AEC-Q100/UL1577 qualification travelers. Point one at a power supply spec, a PI Expert™ schematic or a gate driver test log and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
