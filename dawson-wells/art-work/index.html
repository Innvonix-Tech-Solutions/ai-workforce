A layered study in earth pigment, Umber Field, No. 4 holds its weight quietly. Built from successive washes of oil on linen, the surface shifts between warmth and shadow depending on where the light falls and where the viewer stands.

It is a work made to be seen slowly, and to reward the second look. Additional notes, exhibition history and condition details appear here where applicable.
