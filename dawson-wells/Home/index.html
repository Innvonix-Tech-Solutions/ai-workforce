Mission & Vision

A gallery built on presentation, storytelling and the close encounter.

Dawson Wells Gallery exists to give modern and contemporary work the setting it deserves. We are not a marketplace. We are a place to see, to consider, and to return to — online first, and then in person.

Every work in the collection is chosen for the conversation it starts. Our purpose is to make that conversation reach a global audience, and to bring that audience through our doors.
