Each AI operator is a Speedster®7t FPGA design, Speedcore™ eFPGA IP core sizing, or VectorPath® accelerator engineering workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. Achronix Semiconductor Corporation (headquartered in Santa Clara, California) is a premier fabless semiconductor pioneer and the only commercial supplier offering both high-performance standalone FPGAs and embedded FPGA (eFPGA) intellectual property. Achronix pioneers Speedster®7t FPGAs with revolutionary 2D Network-on-Chip (2D NoC) crossbars delivering >20 Tbps bandwidth, high-density Machine Learning Processors (MLPs), 4.1 Tbps GDDR6 memory controllers, VectorPath® accelerator cards, and Speedcore™ eFPGA IP for custom ASICs and chiplets. One track drives commercial sourcing & Tier-1 system relationships: it captures Data Center Hyperscalers (AWS, Microsoft, Google, Meta), Telecom & 5G/6G OEMs (Ericsson, Nokia), Aerospace & Defense Prime Contractors (Lockheed Martin, Raytheon), and FinTech HFT Firms (Citadel, Jump Trading), quotes VectorPath VP815 accelerator cards (500 units/quarter), structures Speedcore eFPGA licensing proposals, and highlights Achronix's 5x TCO advantage over GPUs. The other track delivers FPGA, eFPGA & 2D NoC silicon engineering precision: it compiles real-time LLaMA 3.3 70B inference on VectorPath VP815 cards (82.4 TOPS, sub-12ms time-to-first-token), synthesizes 400GbE line-rate packet processing SmartNIC pipelines (0% packet drop at 600 Mpps), optimizes sub-microsecond algorithmic trading cores (21.8 ns wire-to-wire), sizes custom Speedcore eFPGA fabrics for TSMC 5nm ASICs (88% power savings), and certifies UCIe 1.1 chiplet packaging (sub-2ns die-to-die latency). Point one at an AI model spec, a 400G network trace or an eFPGA RTL netlist and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
