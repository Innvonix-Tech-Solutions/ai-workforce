Achronix Semiconductor Corporation, headquartered in Santa Clara, California, is a premier fabless semiconductor pioneer and the only commercial supplier offering both high-performance standalone FPGAs and embedded FPGA (eFPGA) intellectual property. Built on TSMC 7nm FinFET technology and leading advanced packaging architectures, Achronix products solve high-bandwidth computing bottlenecks for AI/ML inference, 5G/6G wireless infrastructure, data center networking, and military radar systems. Key architectural breakthroughs include the Speedster®7t FPGA family featuring a revolutionary 2D Network-on-Chip (2D NoC) delivering over 20 Tbps of crossbar bandwidth, high-density Machine Learning Processors (MLPs) supporting integer and floating-point numeric formats, 4.1 Tbps GDDR6 memory controllers, and PCIe Gen5 / 400GbE interfaces. The flagship VectorPath® 815 accelerator card and Achronix AI Console unlock real-time inference for large language models (LLaMA 3.3) and speech-to-text with 5x total cost of ownership advantages over GPUs, while Speedcore™ eFPGA IP embeds programmable logic directly into custom ASICs and chiplets to slash power by 88%.
