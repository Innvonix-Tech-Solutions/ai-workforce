Independent concept  ·  illustrative
Each AI operator is a mass transit ticketing, airline RFID baggage, ePassport biometrics, or RTLS tracking workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track closes commercial deals: it prospects municipal transit authorities (MTA, RATP, TfL), airlines & airports (IATA Resolution 753), hospital RTLS buyers, quotes Calypso smartcards, RAIN RFID labels & metal payment cards, responds to public tenders, and tracks contactless EMV market trends. The other track delivers engineering & quality: it designs custom RFID antenna inlays, audits DESFire EV3 & CoreLam® polycarbonate ePassport tamper resistance, diagnoses hospital RTLS beacon performance, and plans silicon wafer & chip module supply chains. Point one at a transit tender, an RFID antenna CAD design, or an airport reader fault, and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for engineering or sales directors.
