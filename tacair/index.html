Each AI operator is a Tactical Air Support (TacAir) commercial adversary air (ADAIR), Topgun JTAC close air support, F-5AT Advanced Tiger supersonic flight operations, or FAA Part 145 J85 depot overhaul workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track wins the work: it finds USAF fighter wings (Nellis, Eglin, Luke), US Navy strike fighter squadrons (NAS Fallon, NAS Oceana), and Allied MoDs seeking high-threat Red Air combat training, quotes turn-key 3,000-flight-hour F-5AT adversary detachments, responds to US Navy PMA-226 master specialized aircraft tenders, and tracks defense aviation market intelligence. The other track delivers tactical aviation engineering, MRO and flight operations: it qualifies supersonic F-5AT aircraft under NAVAIR, DCMA 8210.1C, & FAA Part 145 standards (Garmin 3000 integrated glass flight deck, Duotech Nemesis pulse-Doppler radar, structural life extension SLEP, zero airframe micro-defects), optimizes automated J85-GE-21 afterburning turbojet depot test cell thrust yields (5,000 lbf afterburning thrust, turbine inlet temp <960°C), simulates pressurized nose radome & avionics bay 3D CFD thermal management (<55°C during continuous Mach 1.4 dash at 35,000 ft), and manages NAVAIR Military Flight Release (MFR) ECOs & tactical datalink software revisions. Point one at a DoD flight RFP, an ACMI 3D air combat telemetry file, or an FAA Part 145 engine borescope report and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a VP of Flight Operations, Chief Test Pilot, or Director of Maintenance to approve.
