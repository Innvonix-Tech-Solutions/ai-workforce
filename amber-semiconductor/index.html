Amber Semiconductor (AmberSemi) is a pioneering fabless semiconductor company headquartered in Dublin, California, led by Founder & CEO Thar Casey. AmberSemi is addressing the AI-driven data center energy crisis head-on through breakthrough solid-state silicon power architectures. Its flagship PowerTile™ Vertical Power Delivery (VPD) solution mounts directly on the underside of server motherboards beneath 1,000W+ AI processors (such as Nvidia Blackwell, AMD Instinct, and custom hyperscaler ASICs), delivering 1,000A per chip in an ultra-low 1.68mm profile and reducing motherboard power distribution loss by over 85%. Complementing PowerTile, AmberSemi's foundational silicon IP includes AC Direct DC conversion (extracting regulated DC power without bulky magnetics or electrolytic capacitors) and sub-microsecond solid-state digital circuit breakers. This interactive AI workforce prototype simulates autonomous multi-agent co-design across vertical power delivery, PDN impedance extraction, GaN-on-Si integration, and OCP Open Rack v3 qualification.
