Independent concept  ·  illustrative
Each AI operator is a BlueWave commercial land acquisition, agrivoltaics site engineering, SMART ASTGU dual-use incentive qualification, BESS battery storage dispatch, or community solar offtake workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track originates, finances, and structures community solar and dual-use development: it scouts agricultural landowners, cranberry bogs, and cattle pastures across Massachusetts, Maine, and the Mid-Atlantic, models 20-year land leases with agricultural coexistence agreements, structures utility PPA and corporate offtake master tenders, and benchmarks community solar credit subscriber acquisition costs. The other track delivers agronomic engineering, dual-use solar layout optimization, and B-Corp environmental stewardship: it qualifies projects under Massachusetts DOER SMART Agricultural Solar Tariff Generation Unit (ASTGU) standards (10-ft tracker clearances, 50% solar sunlight transmission models, rotational sheep grazing densities), designs paired lithium-ion battery energy storage systems (BESS), models microclimatic soil moisture retention under elevated panels, and enforces ISO/ASTM engineering change orders for racking and bifacial PV modules. Point one at a parcel GIS boundary, an ISO-NE interconnection cluster study, or an agronomic shading simulation and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a Chief Development Officer, VP of Agrivoltaics, or Director of Asset Management to approve.
