Each AI operator is a 60 GHz mmWave semiconductor commercial or RF engineering workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. Peraso Inc. (NASDAQ: PRSO, San Jose CA) is a global leader in unlicensed 60 GHz mmWave technology (57-71 GHz full-band support). One track drives commercial sourcing & FWA expansion: it captures Wireless Internet Service Providers (WISPs), 5G FWA equipment OEMs, and tactical military defense contractors needing X720/X130 chipsets and Perspectus/Versatus modules, quotes high-volume 60 GHz orders, structures military drone IFF (Identification Friend or Foe) mesh communication proposals, and highlights Peraso's 25km+ extended FWA reach and IEEE 802.11ad/ay standard leadership. The other track delivers RFIC & phased-array module engineering precision: it designs 60 GHz outdoor FWA antenna-in-package modules (+42 dBm EIRP, 10 Gbps throughput), optimizes PRS4601-B2E baseband MAC/PHY beam-steering firmware (<10μs switching takt time), designs compact tactical drone IFF modules, and audits automated Over-The-Air (OTA) chamber testing at OSAT packaging facilities. Point one at an FWA link budget, a 60 GHz phased-array schematic or an OTA test log and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
