Each AI operator is a 2K x 2K OLED-on-Silicon (OLEDoS) microdisplay backplane, pancake optical module, or F-35 Joint Strike Fighter helmet-mounted display system (HMDS) engineering workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. Kopin Corporation (NASDAQ: KOPN, headquartered in Westborough, Massachusetts) is the premier developer of high-resolution microdisplays (MicroOLED, MicroLED, OLED-on-Silicon, CyberDisplay® AMLCD), optical modules (pancake optics, free-form prisms, spatial light modulators), and sub-systems for defense (F-35 HMDS helmet displays, thermal weapon sights, IVAS), medical surgical displays, industrial AR/VR headsets, and AI optical interconnects. One track drives commercial sourcing & defense prime relationships: it captures Defense Primes (Collins Aerospace, Elbit Systems, BAE Systems, Raytheon), Medical Device OEMs (Intuitive Surgical, Stryker), and Enterprise AR/VR Builders, quotes 2K x 2K MicroOLED display engines (35,000+ nits, 50,000 units/year), structures F-35 HMDS display upgrade proposals, and highlights Kopin's sole-source F-35 defense heritage, high-brightness MicroOLED lead, and custom optical sub-system integration. The other track delivers microdisplay & defense optical semiconductor engineering precision: it designs 2K x 2K 0.9" OLEDoS microdisplay backplanes (38,500 nits peak white, 100,000:1 contrast ratio, 120 Hz refresh rate), optimizes compact pancake optical modules (MTF > 0.55 at 50 lp/mm, ghosting < 0.15%), verifies MIL-STD-3009 NVIS Class B night-vision compatibility, and audits Westborough MA wafer probe & optical ATE lines (luminance uniformity Cpk = 1.91). Point one at an OLEDoS silicon spec, a pancake optics ray-trace log or an NVIS night-vision trace and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
