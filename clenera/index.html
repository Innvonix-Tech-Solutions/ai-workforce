One track wins the work: it finds Western investor-owned utilities (Salt River Project, PNM, NV Energy, Idaho Power) and corporate clean energy buyers seeking 400 MW+ utility-scale solar and 4-hour BESS power purchase agreements (PPAs), quotes turn-key 1,200 MW solar + 1,000 MWh BESS utility PPA & capacity tolling agreements (like CO Bar Solar), responds to multi-gigawatt Southwest clean power authority tenders, and tracks North American utility solar & BESS developer market intelligence. Point one at a utility PPA RFP, a WECC interconnection study, or a CO Bar SCADA telemetry feed and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a VP of Development, Chief Operations Officer, or Director of Asset Management to approve.
