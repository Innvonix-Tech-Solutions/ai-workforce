AI operators that automate hot foil stamping die intake, CNC/etching scheduling, Jobsaver plate ROI, & foil finishing consultation
Independent concept  ·  Innvonix
Dreyer Kliche (kliche.nu) crafts CNC brass and etched magnesium hot foil stamping dies and embossing tools from Copenhagen, alongside metallic, pigment, and special foil ranges. Each AI operator below is a real workflow turned into a fixed team of specialist agents. Track 1 handles commercial intake & client consultation: it parses artwork files, quotes combination dies, recommends metallic & holographic foils, and identifies repeat orders for Jobsaver plate savings. Track 2 delivers production & compliance engineering: it routes jobs through CNC brass machining or magnesium etching, runs dimensional QA against artwork specs, and audits environmental process compliance. Point an operator at a client artwork file, reorder request, or finishing query, and it runs the end-to-end loop to produce a review-ready output.
