Each AI operator is a SpecLab 3D CFD facility airflow simulation, 24ft Powerfoil direct-drive layout, or BAFCon BACnet destratification workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. Big Ass Fans (headquartered in Lexington, Kentucky) is the undisputed pioneer and global technology leader in High-Volume, Low-Speed (HVLS) fans, intelligent architectural cooling, and industrial air movement systems. Engineered on aircraft-grade aerodynamics and heavy-duty direct-drive permanent magnet motors, Big Ass Fans delivers transformational airflow across mega-warehouses, automotive plants, logistics hubs, and commercial spaces. One track drives commercial sourcing & turnkey facility quoting: it uncovers enterprise logistics and industrial manufacturing expansion programs (Amazon, FedEx, Toyota, Walmart), calculates localized utility energy efficiency rebates, structures turnkey equipment CapEx packages, and tracks global HVLS competitive dynamics. The other track executes SpecLab 3D computational fluid dynamics (CFD) simulation, aerodynamic airfoil optimization, building automation, and life-safety engineering: it models 3D airflow velocity fields and thermal destratification in facilities up to 1,000,000 sq ft, calculates Airfence winglet thrust dynamics, integrates BAFCon BACnet/Modbus smart climate controllers, and qualifies NFPA 13 early-suppression fire relay interlocks. Point one at a facility architectural drawing, an HVAC energy bill, or an ambient heat stress report and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
