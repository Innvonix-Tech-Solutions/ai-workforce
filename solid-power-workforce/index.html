AI operators that prospect automotive OEMs, quote sulfide electrolyte supply, qualify A-sample ASSB cells, & audit nail-penetration safety
Independent concept  ·  illustrative
Each AI operator is an automotive OEM partnership or all-solid-state battery (ASSB) engineering workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track closes commercial OEM licensing & supply contracts: it prospects global EV automakers (BMW, Ford, SK On, Hyundai), quotes metric-ton sulfide electrolyte supply & cell tech transfer agreements, responds to OEM RFIs with volumetric energy density (>900 Wh/L) data, and tracks solid-state battery market moves. The other track powers pilot line R&D & qualification: it qualifies silicon-anode A-sample cell batches, audits thermal runaway & nail penetration safety, diagnoses pilot synthesizer pressure drift, and plans Li2S / P2S5 precursor supply chains. Point one at an OEM RFI, an A-sample cell test log, or a synthesizer telemetry feed, and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for executive approval.
