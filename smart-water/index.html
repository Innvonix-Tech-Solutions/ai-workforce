Each AI operator is an AquaControl® client retention, 24–48h predictive leak ML, or telemetry data pipeline workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. Track 1 focuses on client retention and ML upsell: it identifies deployed commercial real estate (CRE) & utility accounts at risk of churn, and calculates property damage claim savings to justify ML upsell. Track 2 delivers zero-hardware predictive ML engineering: it extracts micro-pressure decay features from existing AquaControl flow streams, trains lightweight 24–48h early leak prediction models, benchmarks ML accuracy against rule-based thresholds, and integrates zero-hardware ML inference pipelines directly into the AquaControl cloud API. Point one at a CRE client account or flow telemetry dataset, and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for Geospace leadership.
