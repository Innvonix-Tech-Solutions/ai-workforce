One track wins the work: it finds semiconductor wafer foundries, aerospace defense contractors, and EV battery material manufacturers seeking SiC crystal growth reactors, CVI systems, and silicon nanowire CVD tools, quotes turn-key equipment agreements, responds to global fab master tenders, and tracks semiconductor equipment market intelligence. Point one at a fab customer account, a vacuum leak test report, or a thermal furnace batch log and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a VP of Equipment Engineering or Director of Quality Assurance to approve.
