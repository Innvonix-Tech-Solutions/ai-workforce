ForwardEdge ASIC is a specialized microelectronics design company and a wholly owned subsidiary of Lockheed Martin, headquartered in Saint Paul, Minnesota. Established in May 2023 under founding CEO Brian Sutterfield and currently led by semiconductor industry veteran Frank Ferrante (former executive at Wolfspeed and Intel Foundry Services), ForwardEdge ASIC bridges the speed and agility of commercial silicon innovation with high-assurance defense-grade security. Operating as an official DMEA-accredited Trusted IC supplier with 100% domestic chain-of-custody and ITAR compliance, ForwardEdge delivers full-stack custom ASIC and FPGA design services, backed by 300+ patents and an industry-leading 87% first-pass silicon success rate. Its breakthrough technology portfolio spans radiation-hardened defense microelectronics, SOSA-aligned 3U VPX ruggedized processing modules, DARPA ERIS and MDA SHIELD defense architectures, next-generation UCIe 3.0 multi-die chiplet IP, and commercial DDR5 SPD5118A0 memory hub and TS5 thermal sensor chipsets co-developed under its specialized Memory Division. This interactive AI workforce prototype simulates autonomous multi-agent orchestration across defense qualification, radiation hardening, chiplet packaging, timing closure, and FPGA-to-ASIC SWaP-C conversion.
