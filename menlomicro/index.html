AI operators that discover 5G & defense OEM accounts, quote Ideal Switch® MEMS modules, validate 10B cycle reliability, audit MEMS fab yields, and automate ATE wafer testing
Independent concept  ·  illustrative
Each AI operator is a commercial or MEMS engineering workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track wins the work: it finds 5G infrastructure, defense radar, and ATE instrumentation OEMs seeking to replace legacy electromechanical relays, quotes Ideal Switch® SP4T/DPDT modules with custom substrates, responds to military RFP tenders, and tracks market intelligence. The other track delivers the MEMS engineering and production: it runs 10-billion cycle reliability audits, optimizes 8-inch glass wafer fab yields, generates ATE wafer probe test vectors, and manages MEMS mask revision ECOs. Point one at an OEM design-in case, an S-parameter dataset, or a MEMS wafer probe file and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for an engineering lead to approve.
