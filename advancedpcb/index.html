Each AI employee is an automated FreeDFM analysis, 40+ layer HDI impedance stackup, Mil-PRF-31032 rigid-flex, or quick-turn SMT assembly workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. AdvancedPCB (headquartered in Aurora, Colorado with facilities across Tempe, Maple Grove, and Anaheim) is North America's premier quick-turn printed circuit board manufacturer. Point one at a Gerber or ODB++ dataset, an RF substrate drawing, an ITAR traveler, or a bill of materials and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for an engineering lead to approve.
