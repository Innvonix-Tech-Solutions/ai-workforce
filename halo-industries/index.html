Independent concept  ·  illustrative
Each AI operator is a Silicon Carbide (SiC) wafering commercial or laser cleavage engineering workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track captures semiconductor business: it finds SiC crystal growers (Wolfspeed, Coherent, Resonac), power IDMs (STMicro, Infineon, Onsemi), and EV power module builders needing kerf-free laser slicing, quotes laser tool systems, structures 30-day proof-of-concept ingot slicing agreements, and quantifies ESG material savings. The other track delivers photonics engineering precision: it tunes sub-surface laser focal plane optics, executes controlled laser micro-cleavage without mechanical sawing (zero kerf loss, +50% more wafers per ingot), measures sub-surface damage (SSD < 0.8µm), optimizes CMP polishing time, and signs off wafer travelers. Point one at an ingot specification, a laser pulse recipe or a CMP metrology log and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
