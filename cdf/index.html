Each AI operator is a CDF Corporation Bag-in-Box (BIB), Form-Fit drum liner, Smart Pouch, or blown film extrusion workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track wins the work: it finds industrial chemical manufacturers (Dow, BASF, Ecolab), beverage syrup processors (Coca-Cola, PepsiCo), and lubricant blenders seeking CDF Bag-in-Box, drum liners, and Smart Pouches, quotes turn-key wholesale liquid packaging contracts, responds to global chemical conglomerate master tenders, and tracks sustainable flexible packaging market intelligence. The other track delivers flexible packaging manufacturing and operations: it qualifies EVOH Bag-in-Box high-barrier film & drum liners under UN/DOT 4G, FDA 21 CFR, & ISO 22000 standards (OTR <0.1 cc/m²·day, 1.2 m drop test pass @ -18°C, burst pressure >250 kPa, zero pinhole leak), optimizes blown film extrusion yields & ultrasonic spout weld inspection (99.6% thickness uniformity, burst pressure >220 kPa), simulates drum liner vacuum forming CFD thermal flow & heat transfer (165°C vacuum mold sheet stretch, corner thickness >12 mil), and manages monomaterial PE film & fitment ECO revisions. Point one at an industrial buyer account, a third-party UN drop lab sheet, or an extrusion telemetry G-code file and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a VP of Supply Chain, Director of Packaging Engineering, or Quality Assurance Director to approve.
