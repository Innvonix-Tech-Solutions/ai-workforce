InnoGrit Corporation is a premier fabless semiconductor company headquartered in San Jose, California, founded in 2016 by Dr. Zining Wu (former CTO of Marvell Technology). InnoGrit delivers high-performance, low-power solid-state drive (SSD) controller ICs and full turnkey (FTK) enterprise storage systems engineered to overcome the data storage wall in the artificial intelligence era. Spanning consumer client AI PCs, hyperscale cloud data centers, and mission-critical edge infrastructure, InnoGrit's technology portfolio features PCIe Gen4/Gen5/Gen6 controllers (including the flagship Tacoma IG5669 and Crestone IG5686), CXL 3.1 memory expanders (Cascade IG5676), ultra-low-latency AI cache drives (InnoGrit-N3X with Kioxia XL-FLASH delivering 13µs latency and 100 DWPD), and proprietary 3rd-generation 4K LDPC error correction. This interactive AI workforce prototype models autonomous multi-agent orchestration across enterprise qualification, silicon PPA optimization, firmware FDP co-design, and exabyte storage TCO analysis.
