Each AI operator is an EBARA Technologies dry vacuum pump (EV-S / EV-M), Point-of-Use (POU) thermal/wet scrubber, F-REX CMP wafer planarization, or cleanroom subfab infrastructure workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track wins the work: it finds 300mm mega-fabs and advanced foundries (TSMC, Intel, Samsung, Micron, GlobalFoundries) seeking EBARA harsh-duty dry vacuum pumps and high-DRE thermal scrubbers, quotes turn-key 150-unit subfab equipment fleets, responds to global semiconductor master infrastructure tenders, and tracks subfab market intelligence. The other track delivers semiconductor subfab quality, lab testing and operations: it qualifies EV-S dry vacuum pumps under SEMI S2/S8 & corrosive etch standards (2,000 hr continuous NF3/Cl2/BCl3 exposure, zero rotor corrosion, helium leak rate <1x10⁻⁸ mbar·l/s), optimizes F-REX 300mm CMP wafer polishing removal rates & sub-angstrom roughness (>4,500 Å/min, Ra <0.8 Å, <15 defect particles @ >20 nm), simulates Point-of-Use thermal abatement combustion chamber 3D CFD thermal dynamics (>1,450°C core temp, >99.99% PFC Destruction and Removal Efficiency, NOx <15 ppm), and manages low-GWP fluorinated gas abatement & Eco-mode pump power-reduction ECO revisions. Point one at a mega-fab RFP, a residual gas analyzer (RGA) test sheet, or an EBARA i-Vision subfab telemetry feed and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a VP of Subfab Engineering, Director of Cleanroom Operations, or Facilities Procurement Lead to approve.
