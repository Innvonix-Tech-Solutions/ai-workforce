RAB Lighting · AI Sales & Specification Workforce
AI operators that design layouts, cross-reference fixtures, find rebates, quote, commission controls and build submittals
Independent concept  ·  illustrative
Each AI operator is a sales or engineering workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track wins the project through the channel: it produces the photometric lighting layout, cross-references a competitor fixture to a RAB equivalent, finds the utility rebate, and quotes the job. The other track supports and delivers: it configures the controls and Lightcloud commissioning, assembles the compliant submittal package, plans inventory across the warehouses, and resolves product support and warranty. Point one at a project, a fixture or a support case and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a lighting designer or rep to approve.
