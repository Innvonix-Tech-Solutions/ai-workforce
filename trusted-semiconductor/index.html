Each AI operator is a defense microelectronics commercial or rad-hard silicon engineering workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track captures defense business: it finds defense primes (Lockheed, Raytheon, Northrop, BAE), space payload integrators, and DoD agencies (Navy, AFRL, MDA) requiring DMEA Category 1A Trusted microelectronics, scopes custom ASIC NRE and tape-outs, evaluates FPGA-to-ASIC conversions, and models ITAR/trusted supply chain compliance. The other track delivers rad-hard silicon engineering: it designs RHBD (Radiation Hardened By Design) cell libraries, executes GDSII tape-outs, runs Heavy-Ion SEE & TID radiation qualification for spaceflight hardware, designs CQFP/CBGA ceramic hermetic packaging, and conducts MIL-STD-883 Class V / Class K screening. Point one at a defense spec, an FPGA netlist or a GDSII layout and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
