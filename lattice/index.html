Independent concept  ·  illustrative
Each AI operator is a low-power FPGA fabric design, sensAI™ Edge AI inference, or Sentry™ hardware security root of trust engineering workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. Lattice Semiconductor Corporation (NASDAQ: LSCC, Hillsboro, Oregon HQ & San Jose CA R&D center) is the global leader in low-power programmable logic devices. One track drives commercial sourcing & industrial/automotive OEM relationships: it captures Industrial Automation & Robotics OEMs (Siemens, Fanuc, ABB, Cognex), Automotive ADAS Suppliers (Bosch, Continental, Aptiv), and Data Center Server OEMs (Dell, HPE, Supermicro, Lenovo), quotes Nexus™ 28nm FD-SOI & Avant™ 16nm FPGA volume orders, structures Sentry™ NIST SP 800-193 PFR server security proposals, and highlights Lattice's 4× lower power consumption, 100× lower soft error rate (SER), and specialized solution stacks. The other track delivers low-power FPGA & solution stack engineering precision: it designs 28nm FD-SOI Nexus™ CrossLink™-NX embedded vision FPGA fabrics (40K LUTs, dual 2.5 Gbps MIPI CSI-2 channels, 150 mW total power), compiles MobileNetV2 models onto Avant™-E mid-range FPGAs (120 fps at 850 mW), verifies MachXO5™-NX platform firmware resiliency (PFR) crypto engines (ECDSA-384 signature verification < 3.2 ms), and audits Hillsboro OR / TSMC wafer prober & bitstream test yields (Cpk = 1.86). Point one at an FPGA RTL design, a sensAI model weight file or a server security specification and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
