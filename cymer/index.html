Each AI operator is an XLR ArF dual-chamber MOPA laser calibration, EUV droplet plasma synchronization, or OnPulse® predictive maintenance workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. Cymer, an ASML company headquartered in San Diego, California, is the global leader in excimer laser illumination and deep ultraviolet (DUV) light sources, as well as the fundamental pioneer behind laser-produced plasma (LPP) extreme ultraviolet (EUV) light source technology. Cymer light sources power the world's most advanced semiconductor photolithography scanners, including the ASML TWINSCAN NXT immersion series and NXE/EXE EUV platforms, enabling foundries and IDMs to mass-produce chips down to sub-2nm nodes. One track drives commercial sourcing & OnPulse fleet availability: it captures Leading Foundries & IDMs (TSMC, Intel, Samsung, Micron, SK Hynix), structures multi-year OnPulse cost-per-billion-pulse subscription contracts (>99.8% tool availability SLA), prices XLR/ELS excimer chamber overhauls, and tracks competitive light source metrics. The other track delivers DUV optics, EUV plasma & sub-fab engineering precision: it stabilizes XLR 1000ix dual-chamber MOPA E95 bandwidth below 0.22 pm at 6,000 Hz, co-tunes dynamic pulse energy with TWINSCAN NXT immersion scanners, synchronizes 50 kHz molten tin droplets with high-power CO2 drive lasers (350W IF power), and models Mo/Si collector mirror debris mitigation with in-situ hydrogen radical etching. Point one at a laser pulse log, an optical bandwidth trace or a sub-fab gas telemetry stream and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
