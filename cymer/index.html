Cymer, an ASML company headquartered in San Diego, California, is the global leader in excimer laser illumination and deep ultraviolet (DUV) light sources, as well as the fundamental innovator behind laser-produced plasma (LPP) extreme ultraviolet (EUV) light source technology. Cymer light sources power the world's most advanced semiconductor photolithography scanners, including the ASML TWINSCAN NXT immersion series and NXE/EXE EUV platforms, enabling leading-edge foundries and IDMs to mass-produce chips down to sub-2nm nodes. Key engineering pillars include XLR Series dual-chamber MOPA (Master Oscillator Power Amplifier) ArF immersion lasers with sub-picometer E95 bandwidth control, high-precision Line Narrowing Modules (LNM), ultra-fast wavemeter metrology, 50 kHz molten tin droplet generators, multi-kilowatt CO2 drive lasers, collector mirror debris mitigation, and the signature OnPulse® subscription availability and predictive maintenance model. This interactive AI workforce prototype simulates autonomous multi-agent orchestration across DUV spectral stabilization, EUV droplet plasma co-alignment, automated halogen gas replenishment, and predictive fab uptime management.
