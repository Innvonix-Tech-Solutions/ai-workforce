Each AI operator is a Flip Electronics obsolescence management workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track drives commercial supply security: scrubbing defense and aerospace program BOMs for EOL risk, quoting Last-Time Buy (LTB) inventory capitalization, building Resurgent licensed manufacturing proposals, and tracking global grey-market counterfeit alerts. The other track executes deep quality and silicon assurance: executing 2,400-line defense BOM obsolescence mitigations, running AS6081 and IDEA-STD-1010 counterfeit lab inspections, qualifying Resurgent legacy wafer banking runs, and auditing AS9120B factory Certificates of Conformance. Point one at a target BOM or component specification and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for human approval.
