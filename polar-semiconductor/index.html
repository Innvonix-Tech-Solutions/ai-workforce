Each AI operator is a 200mm semiconductor wafer tape-out, automotive AEC-Q100 qualification, or pure-play foundry sales workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track closes commercial foundry contracts: it prospects Tier-1 automotive suppliers (Bosch, Denso, Aptiv), EV power inverter OEMs & CHIPS Act defense partners, quotes 200mm BCD & HV-CMOS wafer runs ($1,850/wafer), responds to automotive RFIs with BCD process design kits (PDK), and tracks global power IC market demand. The other track delivers wafer fab & cleanroom engineering: it qualifies BCD 100V DMOS breakdown voltage, audits zero-defect AEC-Q100 Grade 0 reliability, diagnoses cleanroom plasma etch particle drift, and plans 200mm prime silicon substrate & specialty gas supply chains. Point one at a foundry RFI, a wafer tape-out CAD design, or a cleanroom yield drop, and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for fab directors.
