Each AI operator is an Astra™ SL1680 edge AI processor, SYN43756 Wi-Fi 7 combo SoC, or TouchPad™ capacitive sensing engineering workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. Synaptics Incorporated (NASDAQ: SYNA, headquartered in San Jose, California) is a premier developer of human-device interface solutions and AI-native IoT edge semiconductor platforms. Synaptics pioneers Edge AI processors (Astra™ SL-Series), Wi-Fi 7 / Bluetooth 6.0 wireless SoCs, TouchPad™ & ClearPad® capacitive touch ICs, Natural ID® fingerprint biometrics, AudioSmart® far-field voice DSPs, VideoSmart® display processors, and DisplayLink® multi-monitor compression ICs for smart home, automotive, enterprise, and mobile devices. One track drives commercial sourcing & Tier-1 OEM relationships: it captures Laptop OEMs (Lenovo, HP, Dell, ASUS), Smart Home Ecosystem Leaders (Amazon, Google, Apple, Samsung), and Automotive Tier-1s (Bosch, Continental), quotes Astra SL1680 edge AI processor volumes (2.5M units/year), structures DisplayLink multi-monitor docking proposals, and highlights Synaptics' integrated Astra AI platform, low-power Wi-Fi 7 lead, and TouchPad market dominance. The other track delivers IoT, wireless & edge AI semiconductor engineering precision: it designs Astra SL1680 edge AI SoCs (quad-core ARM Cortex-A55, 6.0 TOPS NPU, dual-ISP vision, energy efficiency 3.8 TOPS/W, latency < 8.5 ms), synthesizes SYN43756 tri-band Wi-Fi 7 combo ICs (320 MHz width, 4096-QAM, EVM < -43 dB, throughput 5.8 Gbps), evaluates SmartSense™ force-sensing touch controllers (SNR > 45 dB under 100V/m EMI noise), and audits San Jose CA ATE wafer probe lines (NPU clock Cpk = 1.91, probe time 0.22s/die). Point one at an Astra NPU spec, a Wi-Fi 7 RF log or a TouchPad noise trace and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
