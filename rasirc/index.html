Each AI operator is a BRUTE® Hydrazine low-temperature ALD nitride, BRUTE® Peroxide high-k gate oxide, or RainMaker® ultrapure steam delivery workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. RASIRC (headquartered in San Diego, California) is a semiconductor chemical vapor delivery pioneer founded by the late Jeffrey Spiegelman and led by CEO Rob Torres. RASIRC develops patented non-porous fluoropolymer membrane technologies that convert low-vapor-pressure, hazardous liquid chemistries into stable, ultra-pure, anhydrous dynamic gas streams for sub-3nm logic and 200+ layer 3D NAND fabrication. One track drives commercial sourcing & OEM tool co-design: it captures Top Foundries & IDMs (TSMC, Intel, Samsung, Micron) and Tool OEMs (Applied Materials, Lam Research, Tokyo Electron, ASM), quotes bulk chemical precursor cylinder supply, structures Peroxidizer OEM tool integration agreements, and analyzes Raoult's Law vapor delivery advantages. The other track delivers ALD/ALE chemistry, membrane vaporization & sub-fab safety precision: it synthesizes sub-350°C metal nitrides (TiN, TaN, MoN) on Gate-All-Around (GAA) nanosheets without plasma damage, delivers water-free 99.9% pure H2O2 vapor for high-k dielectrics, models 100:1 aspect ratio 3D NAND deep trench conformal coverage, and verifies catalytic pyrophoric gas abatement. Point one at a precursor flow spec, an ALD chamber recipe or a sub-fab toxic gas sensor trace and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
