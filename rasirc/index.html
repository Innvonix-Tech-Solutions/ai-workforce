RASIRC is an advanced semiconductor chemical vapor delivery and equipment pioneer headquartered in San Diego, California, founded by the late industry visionary Jeffrey Spiegelman and led by CEO Rob Torres. RASIRC develops groundbreaking technologies that convert low-vapor-pressure, hazardous reactive liquid chemistries into stable, ultra-pure, anhydrous dynamic gas streams for sub-3nm semiconductor fabrication. Its proprietary non-porous fluoropolymer membrane technology overcomes Raoult's Law limitations, delivering particle-free vapors with sub-ppb metallic purity. Key innovations include BRUTE® Hydrazine (enabling thermal atomic layer deposition of metal nitrides at temperatures under 350°C for Gate-All-Around nanosheets and 3D NAND without plasma damage), BRUTE® Peroxide and the Peroxidizer® (generating water-free 99.9% pure H2O2 gas for high-k dielectrics and atomic cleaning), and RainMaker® ultrapure water vapor systems. Operating in strategic alignment with top-tier tool OEMs (Applied Materials, Lam Research, Tokyo Electron, ASM) and leading semiconductor fabs (TSMC, Intel, Samsung, Micron), RASIRC enables extreme high-aspect-ratio scaling and safe sub-fab chemical delivery. This interactive AI workforce prototype simulates autonomous multi-agent orchestration across low-temperature ALD nitride synthesis, high-k oxidation, 3D NAND trench filling, and fab pyrophoric gas safety abatement.
