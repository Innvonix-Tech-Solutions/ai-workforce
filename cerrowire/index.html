Cerrowire · AI Commercial & Operations Workforce
AI operators that find projects, price copper-indexed bids, plan production across four plants, and keep distributors supplied
Independent concept  ·  illustrative
Each AI operator is a commercial or operations workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track wins the work: it finds construction projects and distributor demand, turns a bid or wire schedule into a copper-indexed quote, keeps the price book aligned to the metal market, and runs competitor intelligence. The other track delivers the work: it plans production across the Alabama, Georgia, Indiana and Utah plants, procures copper and manages inventory, fulfills and ships distributor orders, and checks every product against NEC and UL. Point one at a bid, a price move or an order and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.

Stand up a new AI operator in three steps: pick the workflow, assign each task to an agent, then name it and set the cadence. It joins the workforce.

It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or built on Cerrowire, Marmon or Berkshire Hathaway systems or data. All operators and outputs are illustrative simulations; projects, prices, copper indices, plants, lead times and inventory are placeholders. A human estimator, planner or manager reviews and approves every quote, price change, production plan, shipment and compliance check.
