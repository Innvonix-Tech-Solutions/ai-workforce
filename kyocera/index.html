AI operators that find design-in opportunities, configure LaserLight™ modules, qualify automotive PPAP, build LiFi solutions, and enable OEM integrations
Independent concept  ·  illustrative
Each AI operator is a commercial or engineering workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track captures the design wins: it finds automotive OEMs, Tier-1 suppliers and industrial integrators looking for high-brightness laser illumination, LiFi communication or direct blue-laser processing, qualifies the opportunity, configures the right LaserLight™ SMD or GaN chip, and responds to RFQs. The other track delivers the product: it maps the laser source to the application (headlight, LiFi, industrial), runs thermal and optical simulations, qualifies the module to IATF 16949 and PPAP, runs reliability testing, and packages the reference design. Point one at an OEM requirement, a wavelength spec or an application brief and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
