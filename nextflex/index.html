NextFlex (San Jose, California) is America's Flexible Hybrid Electronics (FHE) Manufacturing USA Institute. Headquartered in Silicon Valley, NextFlex advances domestic electronics manufacturing through dual-use DoD innovation, public-private consortium partnerships, the FlexFactor® workforce development program, and an ITAR & FDA compliant end-to-end FHE pilot manufacturing facility. This prototype demonstrates an autonomous AI workforce automating Project Call proposals, additive roll-to-roll (R2R) printing, conductive nanomaterial ink curing, ultra-thin silicon die attachment, and MIL-STD-810H environmental reliability testing.
