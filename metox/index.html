MetOx International manufactures breakthrough High-Temperature Superconducting (HTS) wire, branded as Xeus™, using advanced reel-to-reel MOCVD epitaxy in Houston, Texas. Capable of carrying over 250 times the current of equivalent copper wire with zero electrical resistance, Xeus™ enables commercial magnetic fusion reactors, gigawatt urban underground power grids, and hyper-dense AI data centers. This prototype demonstrates an AI Workforce of 12 autonomous specialist agents driving commercial fusion magnet procurement, A-MOCVD deposition rate tuning, and cryogenic critical current ($I_c$) metrology.
