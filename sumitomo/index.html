Each AI operator is an advanced engineering polymer or semiconductor packaging materials workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track captures semiconductor fab & 5G/6G OEM business: it finds OSAT packaging houses (ASE, Amkor, JCET), semiconductor fabs (Intel, TSMC, Samsung), and 5G mmWave antenna OEMs needing SUMIKASUPER™ Liquid Crystal Polymer (LCP), SUMIKAEXCEL™ PES, and epitaxial wafers, quotes resin compounding runs, structures high-frequency LCP substrate trial proposals (-65% dielectric loss), and highlights SumiChem's high-flow polymer advantages. The other track delivers material synthesis precision: it formulates 28 GHz / 39 GHz 5G mmWave LCP films ($D_k = 2.85$, $\tan \delta = 0.0008$), optimizes 0.2mm fine-pitch BGA socket injection molding (350°C melt, zero flash), tunes GaAs/GaN MOCVD epitaxial growth at the Phoenix AZ facility (<100/cm² defect density), and signs off JEDEC MSL-1 reflow certificates. Point one at a resin spec, a connector molding drawing or a wafer defect log and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
