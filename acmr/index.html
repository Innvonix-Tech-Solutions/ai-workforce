AI operators that discover semiconductor foundries, quote SAPS & Ultra ECP tools, qualify 300mm megasonic cleaning, test TSV copper plating, and simulate furnace thermal CFD
Independent concept  ·  illustrative
Each AI operator is a semiconductor wafer fabrication equipment (WFE) or cleanroom manufacturing workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track wins the work: it finds tier-1 semiconductor foundries, 3D NAND/DRAM chipmakers, and OSAT advanced packaging facilities seeking SAPS megasonic cleaning and Ultra ECP plating equipment, quotes multi-chamber WFE suites, responds to global memory fab tenders, and tracks 2nm/3nm wet process adoption. The other track delivers the cleanroom process engineering and manufacturing operations: it qualifies SAPS V 300mm megasonic cleaning for 19nm particle removal efficiency (PRE >99.2%), optimizes Ultra ECP TSV copper plating thickness uniformity (<1.8% non-uniformity), simulates 1,050°C Ultra Furnace 300mm thermal CFD dynamics, and manages cleanroom robot arm ECO revisions. Point one at a fab customer account, a KLA wafer defect scan report, or a tool CAD file and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a VP of Fab Process Integration or Director of WFE Operations to approve.
