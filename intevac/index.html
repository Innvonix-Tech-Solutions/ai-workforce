Each AI operator is a physical vapor deposition (PVD) magnetron sputtering, VERTEX® 200mm HDD magnetic media, TRIO™ reactive glass coating, or Diamond-Like Carbon (DLC) workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track wins the work: it finds hard disk drive (HDD) media manufacturers, consumer electronics display cover glass coaters, and solar cell fabs seeking VERTEX® and TRIO™ PVD systems, quotes turn-key equipment agreements, responds to global media fab master tenders, and tracks thin-film market intelligence. The other track delivers equipment manufacturing and plant operations: it qualifies VERTEX® 200mm magnetic layer thickness (12.5 nm ±0.15 nm, coercivity 5,200 Oe, DLC hardness 42 GPa), optimizes UHV magnetron sputtering chamber assembly & target burn-in yield (<2x10^-10 mbar L/s leak rate), simulates PVD magnetron plasma CFD thermal fluid kinetics (±1.0% film thickness uniformity), and manages system spec ECO revisions. Point one at a media fab customer account, a UHV helium leak test report, or a sputtering power batch log and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a VP of Thin-Film Engineering or Director of Quality Assurance to approve.
