AI operators that discover SiC fabs, quote TORRENT wet etch systems, qualify SiC wafer cleanliness, test reman tool yields, and simulate dispense cup CFD
Independent concept  ·  illustrative
Each AI operator is a semiconductor wet processing, photoresist coat/develop track, or remanufactured fab equipment workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track wins the work: it finds 150mm & 200mm SiC/GaN power fabs, MEMS sensor foundries, and advanced packaging OSATs seeking single-wafer wet etch, wafer clean, and coat/develop equipment, quotes turnkey semiconductor tool programs, responds to global fab master tenders, and tracks compound semiconductor capacity expansion. The other track delivers the equipment engineering and fab operations: it qualifies TORRENT 200mm SiC single-wafer wet etch rate & particle cleanliness (<10 particles @ >30nm), optimizes remanufactured TEL/KLA track refurbishing yield (<0.02mm end-effector repeatability), simulates single-wafer chemical dispense cup CFD fluid dynamics (zero splashback @ 2,500 RPM), and manages etch chamber fluidic manifold ECO revisions. Point one at a fab customer account, a wafer defect inspection report, or a SECS/GEM PLC file and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a VP of Fab Operations or Director of Semiconductor Equipment Engineering to approve.
