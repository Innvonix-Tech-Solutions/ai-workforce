Each AI operator is a custom 3-phase Wye power balancing, high-current DC busbar distribution, or AS9100D qualification workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. Interact Power, Inc. (headquartered in Anaheim, California) is a precision engineering and manufacturing pioneer in COTS and custom rack-mount Power Distribution Units (PDUs) for aerospace defense primes, semiconductor Automated Test Equipment (ATE), and heavy industrial mission-critical applications. Built under stringent AS9100D and ISO 9001:2015 quality frameworks, Interact Power units power flight-line radar shelters, satellite ground support systems, semiconductor fab test stands, and defense ground telemetry shelters. One track drives commercial sourcing & defense fast-track quoting: it uncovers program-level requirements across aerospace primes (Lockheed Martin, Boeing, Northrop Grumman, Collins Aerospace, Blue Origin) and ATE leaders (Intel, Teradyne), models expedited 6-week custom turnaround schedules, and structures comprehensive test stand proposals. The other track executes electrical systems engineering, SolidWorks 3D chassis modeling, True-RMS branch metering firmware, and environmental qualification: it balances 3-phase 120/208V and 400-415V loads within 2.5%, integrates 80kA TVSS transient surge protection and MIL-STD-461 EMI filters, provisions 48VDC/270VDC 400A busbar networks, and automates 1500VAC Hi-Pot dielectric travelers. Point one at a defense prime RFQ, a rack thermal envelope, or a ground test specification and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
