The other track executes deep chiplet silicon engineering: co-designing SPHBM multi-die interfaces over low-cost organic substrates, synthesizing 64 Gbps Simultaneous Bidirectional (SBD) PHY transceivers in TSMC 3nm, verifying UCIe 2.0 / BoW protocol interoperability, and validating sub-0.5 pJ/bit channel energy. Point one at a target System-in-Package specification and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for human approval.
