Eliyan Corporation is a leading semiconductor chiplet interconnect company headquartered in Santa Clara, California, backed by Intel Capital, SK hynix, Micron Ventures, and Samsung Catalyst Fund. Founded by the inventors of the Open Compute Project (OCP) Bunch of Wires (BoW) standard, Eliyan eliminates the advanced packaging bottleneck with its patented NuLink™ PHY technology. By delivering advanced packaging bandwidth, latency, and power efficiency over standard organic substrates — and enabling full-duplex Simultaneous Bidirectional (SBD) signaling at up to 64 Gbps/bump — Eliyan shatters the memory wall for generative AI, GPU clusters, and hyperscale accelerators.
