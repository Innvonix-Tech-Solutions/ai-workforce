Each AI operator is a synthetic CVD diamond, quantum NV-center, or high-power thermal/laser window engineering workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. Element Six (De Beers Group — Didcot UK R&D hub, Shannon Ireland main manufacturing fab, Santa Clara CA tech center) is the world leader in synthetic diamond and tungsten carbide supermaterials. One track drives commercial partnerships & quantum/semiconductor sourcing: it captures GaN RF Semiconductor OEMs (Qorvo, Macom, NXP), Quantum Sensing Labs (IBM Quantum, Harvard, Quantum Brilliance), and High-Power Industrial Laser OEMs (Trumpf, IPG Photonics), quotes CVD diamond thermal heat spreaders ($>2,000\text{ W/mK}$), structures quantum-grade DNV-B1™ NV-center research proposals, and highlights E6's microwave plasma CVD reactors, Shannon Ireland fab scale, and De Beers ESG heritage. The other track delivers CVD diamond synthesis & optical polishing precision: it grows single-crystal $^{12}\text{C}$ quantum diamond wafers ($T_2 > 240\mu\text{s}$ spin coherence), fabricates $2,150\text{ W/mK}$ CVD diamond thermal heat spreaders for GaN-on-SiC RF amplifiers, polishes 10kW fiber laser optical diamond windows ($Ra < 0.3\text{ nm}$ roughness, absorption $<0.05\%/\text{cm}$), and audits Shannon fab laser dicing & Raman metrology yields. Point one at a CVD reactor recipe, a quantum NV test log or a thermal heat spreader drawing and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
