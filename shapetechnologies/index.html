Each AI operator is a multi-robot 3D waterjet trimming cell, 94,000 psi HyperJet pump sizing, or 5-axis dynamic kerf taper optimization workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. Shape Technologies Group (headquartered in Kent, Washington) is the undisputed global manufacturing technology leader in ultrahigh-pressure (UHP) process solutions and turnkey robotic automation. Bringing together premier global brands including Flow Waterjet, Shape Process Automation (SPA), KMT Waterjet, and Advanced Pressure Systems (APS), Shape delivers intelligent, cold-cutting manufacturing cells that operate at pressures exceeding 94,000 psi without heat-affected zones (HAZ). One track drives commercial sourcing, turnkey CapEx quoting, and competitive intelligence: it identifies Tier-1 automotive and aerospace primes retooling for lightweight composites and EV structures, configures complex robotic workcell CapEx packages, and tracks global aftermarket UHP seal utilization. The other track executes robotic workcell kinematic simulation, 94k psi hydraulic sizing, Dynamic Waterjet 5-axis taper compensation, and ISO 10218 robotic safety signoff: it balances dual-robot 3D cutting cycles to under 45 seconds, sizes diamond orifices and UHP intensifiers, eliminates composite fiber fraying, and qualifies RIA 15.06 safety systems. Point one at an automotive CAD surface, a titanium bulkhead specification, or a turnkey cell RFQ and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
