Independent concept  ·  illustrative
Each AI operator is a commercial or battery engineering workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track wins the work: it finds electric utilities, independent power producers (IPPs), and data center developers seeking 100-hour long-duration energy storage (LDES), quotes multi-megawatt iron-air battery systems and Formware™ software licenses, responds to utility IRP tenders, and tracks LDES market adoption. The other track delivers the clean-tech engineering and manufacturing: it qualifies Form Factory 1 battery module assembly lines, audits 100-hour continuous charge/discharge cycles, models grid dispatch under extreme weather, and manages battery enclosure ECOs. Point one at a utility account, a 100-hour cycling dataset, or an iron anode CAD file and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for an engineering manager to approve.
