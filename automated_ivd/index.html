Specialized AI operators designed strictly to discover, deeply research, map products, and rate channel partners in Dubai & the UAE
Independent concept  ·  Dubai Focus
Each AI operator is a dedicated Dubai channel partner acquisition & enablement workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track focuses on channel partner discovery and rating: it scans IVD (In Vitro Diagnostics) and medical lab equipment distributors in Dubai, performs deep research on their brand portfolio and lab footprint, maps potential automated microplate product lines (2-plate & 4-plate ELISA systems, microplate washers, reagent consumables), and scores commercial potential. The other track manages partner onboarding and regional enablement: it drafts territory distribution contracts, plans biomedical engineer technical training in Dubai, monitors regional hospital tenders, and audits partner sales targets. Point one at a Dubai distributor or a partner proposal, and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for your team to present over a meeting call.
