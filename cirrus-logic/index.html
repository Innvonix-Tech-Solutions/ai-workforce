Each AI operator is a smart boosted audio amplifier tuning, closed-loop haptics DSP modeling, or mixed-signal automotive silicon qualification workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. Cirrus Logic, Inc. (headquartered in Austin, Texas, NASDAQ: CRUS) is a premier global fabless semiconductor leader pioneering high-performance, low-power mixed-signal processing solutions. Cirrus Logic powers billions of the world's most sophisticated mobile devices, laptops, wearables, and automobiles with flagship audio codecs, boosted class-D smart amplifiers (CS35L56, CS35L45), closed-loop low-latency haptic drivers (CS40L26), camera optical image stabilization (OIS) voice coil motor drivers, and high-efficiency power conversion ICs. One track drives commercial sourcing & Tier-1 mobile, PC and automotive OEM design-wins: it captures smartphone titans (Apple, Samsung, Google), PC leaders (Dell, HP, Lenovo), and automotive innovators (Tesla, BMW, Mercedes-Benz), structures 15-million-unit smart amplifier volume pricing packages, and coordinates multi-year in-cabin road noise cancellation (RNC) platform agreements. The other track executes mixed-signal silicon design, acoustic DSP algorithms & automotive AEC-Q100 reliability: it models real-time speaker voice-coil excursion and thermal protection DSP algorithms (THD+N < -95 dB, > 92% boost converter efficiency), synthesizes resonant frequency tracking for haptic actuators (sub-5ms tactile onset), fine-tunes ultra-low-power hybrid active noise cancellation (sub-5µs acoustic latency), and certifies AEC-Q100 Grade 1 automotive mixed-signal tapeouts. Point one at an acoustic speaker profile, a battery droop specification, or a mixed-signal RTL netlist and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
