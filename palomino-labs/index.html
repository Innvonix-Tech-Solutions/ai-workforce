Palomino Laboratories (palominolabs.ai) is an innovator in high-performance AI hardware interconnects, optical packaging, micro-LED/micro-VCSEL optical engines, high-density copper interconnects, and physical-layer data transmission for hyperscale AI data centers and next-generation GPU/TPU clusters. One track drives commercial sourcing & hardware engine relationships: it captures AI Hyperscalers (Microsoft Azure, AWS, Google Cloud, Meta), GPU Server OEMs (NVIDIA, AMD, Supermicro), and High-Speed Switch Vendors (Arista, Cisco, Broadcom), quotes 3.2T MicroVCSEL optical engine supply contracts (150,000 engine modules/year), structures high-density low-latency copper engine proposals, and highlights Palomino's ultra-low power (< 0.8 pJ/bit), MicroLED/MicroVCSEL integration, and high bandwidth density. The other track delivers AI interconnect & optical packaging engineering precision: it designs 3.2T MicroLED optical interconnect engine arrays (3.2 Tbps throughput, BER < 1E-15, optical eye height > 85%, energy efficiency 0.72 pJ/bit at 224 Gbps/lane), synthesizes 224G PAM4 low-loss copper backplane engines (insertion loss < −18 dB at 56 GHz, crosstalk > 42 dB ICR), evaluates chiplet-to-chiplet PHY energy efficiency (0.68 pJ/bit at 105°C die junction), and audits Palomino optical packaging cleanroom lines (coupling efficiency Cpk = 1.91, probe test takt time 10s/engine). Point one at an optical interconnect spec, a copper insertion loss trace or a PHY energy efficiency log and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
