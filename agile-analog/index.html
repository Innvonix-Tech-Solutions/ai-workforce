Agile Analog is a pioneering semiconductor IP company headquartered in Cambridge, UK, led by CEO Barry Paterson (former executive at Dialog Semiconductor and Maxim Integrated). Agile Analog transforms custom mixed-signal semiconductor development through its revolutionary Composa™ design technology. Rather than relying on manual, multi-month analog schematic drafting and physical layout that must be laboriously re-engineered for every foundry process, Composa programmatically generates custom, highly optimized analog IP from functional sub-circuit building blocks across any foundry node. Its comprehensive portfolio includes agilePMU (power management), agileSECURITY (voltage and clock glitch detection for anti-tamper SoCs), agileSensorIF (12-bit SAR ADCs and DACs), agileIRDROP and agilePVT (process, voltage, temperature tracking for AI and HPC silicon), and agileSMU (always-on sleep management). Operating in partnership with TSMC, Intel Foundry, GlobalFoundries, Samsung, and UMC, Agile Analog compresses 9-month analog design cycles into 3 to 4 weeks. This interactive AI workforce prototype models autonomous multi-agent orchestration across algorithmic analog generation, cross-PDK porting, fault-injection defense verification, and automotive ASIL-D sign-off.
