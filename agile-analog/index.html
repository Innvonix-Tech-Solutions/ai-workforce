The other track executes deep engineering: programmatically synthesizing custom analog IP via Composa, porting macros across FinFET and planar foundry PDKs, verifying anti-tamper glitch defense, and generating automotive ASIL-D safety dossiers. Point one at a target customer SoC spec and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready deliverable for human sign-off.
