AI operators that quote EMS builds, run new-product introduction, size motors, diversify supply, and plan the global floor
Independent concept  ·  illustrative
Each AI operator is a commercial or engineering workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track wins the work: it quotes an EMS build from a customer BOM, finds design and manufacturing programs, sizes a motor or power solution, and runs market intelligence. The other track delivers the work: it runs new-product introduction from concept to scale, diversifies the global supply chain for lowest total cost, builds the quality and regulatory package, and plans production across the sites. Point one at a BOM, a design or the order book and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for an engineer to approve.

Stand up a new AI operator in three steps: pick the workflow, assign each task to an agent, then name it and set the cadence. It joins the workforce.
Independent concept prototype prepared by Innvonix Tech Solutions for a conversation with East West Manufacturing. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or built on East West systems or data. A human engineer or manager reviews and approves every design, BOM, quote, sourcing plan and inspection.
