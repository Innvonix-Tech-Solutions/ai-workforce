Each AI operator is a fault-tolerant photonic quantum computing commercial or silicon photonics engineering workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. PsiQuantum Corp. (Palo Alto HQ, Chicago IL IQMP campus, Brisbane Australia, Malta NY Fab 8) is building the world's first commercially useful, fault-tolerant quantum computer with 1 million physical qubits. One track drives commercial partnerships & enterprise quantum algorithms: it captures Fortune 500 energy, chemical, pharmaceutical, and aerospace partners needing fault-tolerant quantum advantage for catalyst design, solid-state battery electrolytes, and carbon capture, quotes Quantum Compute-as-a-Service (QCaaS) capacity, and highlights PsiQuantum's 300mm semiconductor fab scale with GlobalFoundries and Fusion-Based Quantum Computing (FBQC) error correction. The other track delivers silicon photonics & cryogenic system engineering precision: it designs 300mm silicon photonics chiplets (single-photon source arrays, 45nm CMOS waveguide switches, SNSPD detectors with >98% quantum efficiency), simulates FBQC surface code error thresholds (>1.2% physical error rate tolerance), designs 4 Kelvin cryogenic cooling cabinets (100k physical qubit thermal load), and audits high-volume 300mm wafer probers. Point one at a quantum algorithm circuit, a photonic chip layout or a 4K cryo test log and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
