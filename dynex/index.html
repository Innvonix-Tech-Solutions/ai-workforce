DYNEX Technologies · AI Commercial & Scientific Workforce
AI operators that find labs, onboard ELISA assay protocols, quote instruments and consumables, and support the installed base
Independent concept  ·  illustrative
Each AI operator is a commercial or scientific workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track wins the work: it finds clinical, research and food-safety labs and distributors that need ELISA automation, quotes the DS2, DSX or Agility system with its consumables, responds to tenders, and runs market intelligence. The other track delivers the science and the service: it onboards a customer assay onto the open-system instrument, validates the method and QC, supports the worldwide installed base, and keeps consumables and reagents flowing. Point one at a lab, an assay kit or an instrument in the field and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
