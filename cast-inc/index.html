Each AI operator is a silicon IP core sizing, automotive CAN XL subsystem integration, or ASIL-D functional safety verification workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. CAST, Inc. (headquartered in Woodcliff Lake, New Jersey) is a world-class semiconductor Intellectual Property (silicon IP) pioneer with over 30 years of continuous delivery of production-proven IP cores for ASICs, SoCs, and FPGAs. CAST provides industry-standard silicon solutions across four core pillars: automotive networking (market-leading CAN 2.0, CAN FD, and CAN XL controller cores conforming to ISO 11898-1:2024 and CiA 610-1, LIN, and Automotive Ethernet), embedded RISC-V processors (functional safety ISO 26262 ASIL-D ready EMSA5-FS and BA2X), hardware lossless data compression (ZipAccel Deflate, GZIP, and ZLIB streaming at 100+ Gbps), and hardware security IP (AES-GCM, SHA-3, TRNG, and Post-Quantum Cryptography). One track drives commercial sourcing & silicon IP licensing acceleration: it captures Tier-1 automotive electronics suppliers (Bosch, Continental, Denso, ZF), defense prime contractors, and fabless semiconductor innovators, structures multi-project ASIC RTL and FPGA netlist licensing packages, and formulates zonal gateway IP co-development agreements. The other track executes silicon IP engineering, RTL synthesis & ASIL-D safety signoff: it synthesizes dual-core lockstep RISC-V and CAN XL controller subsystems in TSMC 16nm automotive grade (800 MHz timing closure, 0 CDC violations), runs 100 Gbps ZipAccel streaming compression PPA optimization, executes FMEDA fault injection (SPFM > 99%, LFM > 90%), and certifies SGS-TÜV Saar compliance dossiers. Point one at an automotive ECU specification, a bus architecture profile, or an RTL netlist and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
