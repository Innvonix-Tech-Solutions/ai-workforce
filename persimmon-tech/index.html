Each AI operator is a direct-drive vacuum robot sizing, SwitchBlade cluster tool trajectory optimization, or 3D hybrid-field motor engineering workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. Persimmon Technologies Corporation (headquartered in Bedford, Massachusetts and a group company of Sumitomo Heavy Industries) is a premier global innovator in high-performance vacuum robotics and next-generation electric motor technology for semiconductor manufacturing, advanced packaging, power electronics, and display fabrication. Persimmon pioneers gearless and beltless direct-drive vacuum robots including SwitchBlade (Dual 2-link Yaw), iQuad (Butterfly Quad), iDual (Dual SCARA), and Single SCARA configurations, delivering up to 70% higher motor torque and superior placement repeatability (< ±0.020 mm) in ultra-high vacuum (UHV) semiconductor cluster chambers. One track drives commercial sourcing & Tier-1 OEM tool qualification: it targets semiconductor equipment leaders (Applied Materials, Lam Research, Tokyo Electron, ASML, ASM International, KLA) and global foundries (TSMC, Intel, Samsung), structures 120-unit direct-drive robot volume quotes, and negotiates multi-chamber vacuum transfer module (VTM) integration agreements. The other track executes vacuum robotics, motor physics & cleanroom manufacturing engineering: it synthesizes high-speed dual-wafer SwitchBlade transfer trajectories (> 380 WPH throughput, 0% wafer slip), models 3D hybrid-field soft magnetic composite (SMC) motor flux paths (2x torque density, zero cogging), audits high-vacuum thermal dissipation in corrosive etch/ALD chemistries (fluorine, chlorine, ammonia at 450°C), and signs off ISO Class 1 particle inspection travelers. Point one at a cluster chamber layout, a vacuum kinematic spec, or a motor core parameter and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
