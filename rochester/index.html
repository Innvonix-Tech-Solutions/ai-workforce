AI operators that source EOL chip buyers, quote 15B+ authorized components, re-manufacture obsolete ICs, and manage wafer vaults
Independent concept  ·  illustrative
Each AI operator is a commercial distribution or semiconductor re-manufacturing workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track wins commercial contracts: it finds defense, aerospace, automotive, and industrial OEMs needing active or EOL semiconductors, quotes authorized stock from 15B+ in-stock components, responds to long-term sustainment tenders, and tracks component obsolescence radar. The other track delivers licensed re-manufacturing: it retrieves original wafer lots, assembles & packages obsolete ICs, performs 100% authorized MIL-STD screening, manages nitrogen wafer storage vaults, and ensures AS9100 quality compliance. Point one at an OEM buyer, a part number, or an EOL wafer lot, and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a manager to approve.
