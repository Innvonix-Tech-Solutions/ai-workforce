Independent concept  ·  illustrative
Each AI operator is a commercial or dermatological skincare engineering workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track wins the work: it finds board-certified dermatologists, plastic surgeons, and medical spas seeking authorized ZO® Skin Health practice partnership, quotes practice opening stocking kits, responds to enterprise clinic network tenders, and tracks medical skincare adoption. The other track delivers the clinical engineering and manufacturing: it qualifies 12-week clinical trials and 40°C accelerated stability, optimizes 5,000L cGMP compounding batch yields, audits FDA OTC drug monograph compliance, and manages airless packaging ECOs. Point one at a medical practice account, a 12-week VISIA® trial dataset, or a master batch record and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a medical director to approve.
