Each AI operator is an NDP200 compute-in-memory neural engine design, INT8 ONNX neural network quantization, or NDP120 wake-word acoustic test workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. Syntiant Corp. (headquartered in Irvine, California) is a pioneer in ultra-low-power, always-on "Physical AI" and Neural Decision Processors (NDPs). Featuring custom silicon architectures with compute-in-memory (Syntiant Core 1 & Core 2™), Syntiant enables edge AI processing across audio, speech, vision, sensor fusion, hearables, wearables, smart home, automotive, and industrial IoT. One track drives commercial sourcing & edge OEM relationships: it captures Hearable/Wearable OEMs (Apple Beats, Bose, Sony, Jabra), Smart Home Device Makers (Amazon Ring, Google Nest, Arlo), and Automotive Tier-1s (Bosch, Continental, Mobis), quotes NDP120 audio AI silicon contracts (2.5M IC units/year), structures NDP200 ultra-low-power vision proposals, and highlights Syntiant's native compute-in-memory architecture (< 1 mW always-on operation), sub-10ms wake-word latency, and 20x energy efficiency lead. The other track delivers ultra-low-power edge AI semiconductor & neural processing engineering precision: it designs Syntiant Core 2™ compute-in-memory neural engines (SRAM MAC matrix, 1 MB weight memory, 0.85 mW active power at 0.9V), compiles & quantizes ONNX/TensorFlow models to INT8 (97.4% Top-1 accuracy, 6.2 ms/frame latency), tests NDP120 acoustic wake-word detection (98.8% hit rate at 65 dBA noise, 140 μW always-on, < 8 ms latency), and audits Irvine CA silicon packaging & probe test lines (MAC Vmin Cpk = 1.93, ATE test takt time 4s/chip). Point one at an edge AI model spec, a MAC matrix power trace or an acoustic test log and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
