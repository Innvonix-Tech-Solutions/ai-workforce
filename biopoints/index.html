Type any region and product and a short manufacturer brief. The tool pulls the licensed importer, product-registration and distributor data it can find from public sources, scores each candidate against your weights, and returns a ranked shortlist with the evidence behind every row. It cites what it finds and flags what it cannot verify. You approve the distributors worth approaching.

Run the Distributor Scout

Weights total 100. Normalised automatically.
Reads public sources on demand · cites every field · flags gaps
For each distributor you approved, this builds a Company Approach Brief: what the business is, the why-now signal (and an honest fallback to context when there is no real event), and the opportunity read — is this the right partner for the line and how do you play it. The English and Arabic approach note is written off the brief, not from thin air. You review and edit before anything is sent.
Independent concept prototype built by Innvonix Tech Solutions for a discovery discussion with BioPoints Technologies L.L.C. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or built on any BioPoints system. Company names, snapshots, signals, scores, sources and figures are illustrative placeholders, not real data. Regulatory authorities (SFDA, MOHAP, EDA and others) are shown as examples of the public sources such a tool would read, and must be verified against the authority of record. This is a research-and-brief assistant: it prepares and recommends, and a person reviews and approves every output before it reaches a manufacturer or a distributor.
