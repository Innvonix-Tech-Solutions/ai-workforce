Each AI operator is an E²Stack® EUV spin-on underlayer formulation, BrewerBOND® temporary bonding adhesive synthesis, or ARC® BARC lithography spin-coat evaluation workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. Brewer Science, Inc. (headquartered in Rolla, Missouri & Vichy, Missouri) is a global leader in developing and manufacturing next-generation microelectronic materials, advanced lithography coatings, 2.5D/3D advanced packaging materials, smart devices, and printed sensors. Pioneering Bottom Anti-Reflective Coatings (BARC) for photolithography, Brewer Science develops EUV underlayers (E²Stack®), spin-on carbon (SOC), spin-on hardmasks (SOH), BrewerBOND® temporary bonding/debonding materials, and WaferBOND® materials for 2.5D/3D interposers and ultrathin wafer handling. One track drives commercial sourcing & semiconductor fab relationships: it captures Leading Semiconductor Foundries (TSMC, GlobalFoundries, Intel Foundry, Samsung Foundry), Memory IDMs (Micron, SK Hynix, Samsung), and OSATs (ASE, Amkor), quotes E²Stack EUV underlayer supply contracts (50,000 gallons/year), structures BrewerBOND 3D packaging material proposals, and highlights Brewer Science's invention of BARC, sub-nm EUV line-edge roughness (LER) control, and B-Corp sustainable chemical manufacturing. The other track delivers semiconductor materials & lithography chemical engineering precision: it designs E²Stack EUV spin-on underlayer & SOH formulations (controlled refractive index n=1.72, k=0.38, LWR reduction by 32%, LER < 1.2 nm at 13.5 nm EUV dose 28 mJ/cm²), synthesizes BrewerBOND 305 temporary bonding adhesives (TTV < 1.0 μm across 300mm wafer, thermal stability up to 350°C), evaluates 193nm immersion BARC spin-coat film uniformity (< ±0.4 nm, substrate reflectivity < 0.15%), and audits Vichy MO chemical plant sub-micron filtration lines (trace metal Cpk = 1.93, bottling takt time 120s/gallon). Point one at an EUV underlayer formulation, a temporary bonding shear trace or a BARC spin-coat optical log and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
