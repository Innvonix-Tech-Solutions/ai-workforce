AI operators that qualify foundry programs, translate wafer specs into process recipes, and run yield, capacity and supply
Independent concept  ·  illustrative
Each AI operator is a commercial or manufacturing workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track wins the work: it finds foundry and IDM programs that need 300mm silicon or SiC epi wafers, qualifies the design-in, models the long-term supply agreement, and runs market intelligence. The other track makes the wafer: it translates a customer spec into the crystal-growth, polishing and epitaxy recipe, runs yield and SPC, plans the Sherman fab capacity and ramp, and keeps materials and logistics flowing. Point one at a customer spec, a program or a fab excursion and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for an engineer to approve.
