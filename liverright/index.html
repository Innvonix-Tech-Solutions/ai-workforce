LiveRight · AI Commercial & Virtual Care Workforce
AI operators that expand clinic partnerships, automate telehealth intake, triage liver health risk, and navigate patient care
Independent concept  ·  illustrative
Each AI operator is a commercial or clinical care workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track grows the virtual clinic: it finds primary care clinics, health systems, and high-risk patient leads, automates insurance verification, expands physician referral networks, and runs health awareness. The other track delivers virtual hepatology care: it triages liver disease risk, navigates patient consultation plans, matches patients with clinical trials, and monitors remote patient health metrics. Point one at a patient inquiry, a medical referral, or a care plan, and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a clinician or manager to approve.

Stand up a new AI operator in three steps: pick the workflow, assign each task to an agent, then name it and set the cadence. It joins the workforce.
Independent concept prototype prepared by Innvonix Tech Solutions for a conversation with LiveRight Virtual Liver Disease Clinic. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or built on LiveRight systems or data. All operators and outputs are illustrative simulations; patient records, risk scores, insurance details, care plans, and trial listings are placeholders. A licensed physician, nurse navigator, or clinic manager reviews and approves every patient care plan, clinical triage, trial match, and medical referral. This is an administrative demo tool and does not provide medical diagnosis.
