Each AI operator is a 5-stage commercial payer acquisition or virtual hepatology workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track closes 6-to-7 figure Payer, ACO, and MCO contracts: it ingests CMS ACO public data and CDC liver disease prevalence rates, maps multi-threaded decision units (CMO, VP Pop Health, CFO, VP Contracting), generates role-calibrated outreach anchored in $3,032/mo cirrhosis cost avoidance, orchestrates follow-ups, and prepares pre-meeting briefs for Brandon Tudor & Pete Celano. The other track delivers virtual clinical care: it manages patient intake, FibroScan®/FibroSpect® stratification, board-certified telehepatologist triage, ALT/AST lab normalization tracking, and quality governance. Point one at a target health plan, CMS ACO ID, or patient cohort, and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for leadership approval.
