Each AI operator is an ultra-high-speed SerDes IP commercial or semiconductor physical layer (PHY) engineering workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track captures AI accelerator & hyperscale cloud silicon business: it finds AI chiplet developers (NVIDIA, AMD, Broadcom, Marvell), hyperscale data center builders (AWS, Meta, Google), and 800G/1.6T networking switch vendors requiring 112G/224G PAM4 SerDes and UCIe chiplet interconnects, quotes IP licensing & NRE packages, structures multi-die chiplet platform proposals, and highlights eTopus ADC/DSP latency advantages (<10ns). The other track delivers physical layer silicon precision: it configures 224G PAM4 SerDes IP on 3nm TSMC nodes (BER < $10^{-15}$ at 32dB insertion loss), models 32G UCIe die-to-die chiplet bump pitch (45μm), evaluates PCIe Gen 6/7 64 GT/s PAM4 PHY margin, audits 800G/1.6T backplane channel jitter (RJ < 100fs), and signs off silicon tape-out travelers. Point one at a SerDes channel spec, a chiplet floorplan or an eye diagram simulation log and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
