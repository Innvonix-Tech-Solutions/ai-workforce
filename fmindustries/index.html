AI operators that estimate build-to-print jobs, win programs, plan the floor, run first-article, and protect supply
Independent concept  ·  illustrative
Each AI operator is a commercial or manufacturing workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track wins the work: it estimates a build-to-print package, responds to OEM sourcing programs, and runs market intelligence. The other track makes the work: it plans production across the facilities, builds the first-article and quality package, engineers the machining and coating process, and protects materials and supply. Point one at a drawing, a sourcing package or the order book and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for an estimator or engineer to approve.

Stand up a new AI operator in three steps: pick the workflow, assign each task to an agent, then name it and set the cadence. It joins the workforce.
