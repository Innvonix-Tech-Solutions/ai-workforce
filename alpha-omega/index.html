Each AI operator is an 80A Smart Power Stage (SPS), aSiC™ 1200V Silicon Carbide MOSFET, or AlphaMOS® superjunction power FET engineering workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. Alpha and Omega Semiconductor Limited (NASDAQ: AOSL, Sunnyvale, California HQ) is the global power semiconductor leader integrating discrete MOSFETs, Power ICs, Smart Power Stages (SPS), and SiC power modules. One track drives commercial sourcing & power customer relationships: it captures Hyperscale AI Server Makers (NVIDIA, AMD, Supermicro, Dell, HPE), Automotive EV OEMs (Tesla, Rivian, BYD, Hyundai), and Server PSU Leaders (Delta Electronics, Lite-On), quotes 80A Smart Power Stage volume IC orders, structures aSiC™ 1200V EV onboard charger proposals, and highlights AOS's co-packaged SPS/DrMOS efficiency, proprietary copper-clip packaging, and cost leadership. The other track delivers power semiconductor engineering precision: it designs 80A Smart Power Stage (SPS) ICs (VRM transient dip < 12 mV under 100A/µs load step), simulates aSiC™ 1200V SiC MOSFET switching energy (< 1.2 mJ at 800V/40A), tests 30V AlphaMOS® trench FETs (Rds(on) = 0.65 mΩ max), and audits Oregon fab wafer prober & ATE test yields (BV_dss Cpk = 1.89). Point one at an SPS datasheet spec, a SiC wafer lot profile or an AlphaMOS packaging log and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
