AI operators that license MST® quantum-engineered film IP, optimize transistor TCAD, integrate wafer epitaxy with foundries, and calibrate PDK models
Independent concept  ·  illustrative
Each AI operator is a commercial licensing or semiconductor engineering workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track captures the business: it finds leading foundries, IDMs and fabless chipmakers looking to extend planar, FinFET, GAA, DRAM or GaN-on-Si performance without costly geometry shrinks, qualifies the customer node, structures Joint Development Agreements (JDAs), and models lifetime per-unit royalties. The other track delivers the technology: it optimizes MST® (Mears Silicon Technology) film insertion depths, runs TCAD mobility simulations, plans CMOS epi integration, characterises TEM/SIMS silicon samples, verifies HTOL/BTI reliability, and updates BSIM/SPICE PDK models for customer tape-outs. Point one at a foundry node spec, a JDA draft or an epi profile and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
