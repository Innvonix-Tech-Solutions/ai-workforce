Each AI operator is a single-frequency DBR laser diode commercial or semiconductor photonics engineering workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track captures quantum sensing, atomic clock & aerospace optics business: it finds defense contractors (Lockheed Martin, Raytheon, Northrop Grumman), national quantum research labs (NIST, DARPA, DOE), and atomic clock manufacturers needing Rubidium (780nm/795nm), Cesium (852nm/894nm), Potassium (767nm), and Helium (1083nm) monolithic DBR lasers, quotes volume laser diode orders, structures quantum magnetometer evaluation proposals (-80% size/weight vs ECDL lasers), and highlights Photodigm's 100% U.S. Richardson TX fab advantage. The other track delivers semiconductor photonics precision: it designs 780.24nm Rubidium-D2 DBR laser diodes (150mW output power, ultra-narrow linewidth <500kHz, SMSR > 50dB), configures 1083nm Helium optical pumping DBR wafer fab runs at the Richardson TX facility, assembles 14-pin Butterfly PM fiber-coupled laser modules, and signs off MIL-STD-883 space-grade radiation certificates. Point one at a spectroscopy wavelength requirement, a DBR grating layout or a laser burn-in test log and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
