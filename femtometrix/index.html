AI operators that capture 300mm fab accounts, calibrate Optical Second Harmonic Generation (SHG) lasers, inspect GAA & 3D NAND buried defects, and automate SECS/GEM fab yield sign-off
Independent concept  ·  illustrative
Each AI operator is a 300mm semiconductor metrology commercial or Optical SHG engineering workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track captures fab business: it finds 300mm memory IDMs (Samsung, SK Hynix, Micron) and leading logic foundries (TSMC, Intel Foundry) requiring non-destructive Optical Second Harmonic Generation (SHG) inspection for non-visual defects (NVDs), quotes Harmonic Series™ inline tool systems, structures 90-day fab trial evaluation agreements, and highlights contact-free buried interface inspection advantages. The other track delivers optical metrology precision: it calibrates sub-femtosecond laser pulses (800nm pump to 400nm second harmonic), measures interface trap density ($D_{it} < 1\times 10^{10} \text{ cm}^{-2}\text{eV}^{-1}$), inspects Gate-All-Around (GAA) nanosheet channels and 200+ layer 3D NAND oxide interfaces without wafer destruction, maps EUV overlay strain, verifies SECS/GEM cleanroom automation, and signs off fab yield reports. Point one at a wafer inspection recipe, a fab yield log or an SHG spectrum deck and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
