Each AI operator is a 3D IC DBI® Direct Bond Interconnect hybrid bonding stack, 2.5D silicon interposer with TSV RDLs, or US onshore ITAR defense microelectronics assembly workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. NHanced Semiconductors, Inc. (headquartered in Morrisville, NC & Odon, IN) is the pioneer of "Foundry 2.0" — the leading US-based, ITAR-registered pure-play foundry in advanced 2.5D & 3D IC integration, DBI® hybrid bonding, silicon interposers, chiplets, glass/fused silica substrates, and custom BEoL (Back-End-of-Line) manufacturing for defense, AI/HPC, and aerospace microelectronics. One track drives commercial sourcing & defense prime relationships: it captures Defense & Aerospace Primes (Lockheed Martin, Raytheon, BAE Systems, Northrop Grumman), AI Chiplet Developers (Tenstorrent, Eliyan, AMD), and Fabless OEMs, quotes 3D DBI hybrid bonding & silicon interposer volumes (25,000 wafers/year), structures US onshore ITAR packaging proposals, and highlights NHanced's pure-play Foundry 2.0 model, sub-micron DBI hybrid bonding lead, and US onshore ITAR security. The other track delivers advanced 2.5D/3D packaging semiconductor engineering precision: it designs 3D IC stacks with DBI Cu-Cu hybrid bonding (0.8µm bond pitch, interconnect bandwidth 1.2 Tbps/mm², insertion loss < -0.15 dB at 40 GHz), synthesizes 4-layer Cu RDL 2.5D silicon interposers with 10:1 aspect ratio TSVs, verifies MIL-STD-883 fine/gross leak hermeticity (< 1E-9 atm-cc/sec) and anti-tamper encapsulation, and audits Odon IN / Morrisville NC cleanroom ATE test lines (DBI alignment Cpk = 1.93, alignment < 100 nm). Point one at a 3D stacking spec, a silicon interposer RDL file or an ITAR defense assembly trace and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
