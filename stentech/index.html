AI operators that capture EMS accounts, optimize Gerber CAD DFM, program Fiber Laser cutting, apply BluPrint™ PVD coatings, and fabricate wave solder tooling
Independent concept  ·  illustrative
Each AI operator is an EMS commercial or SMT stencil engineering workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track captures the business: it finds EMS contract manufacturers, automotive/aerospace PCBA providers, and prototype shops needing high-precision SMT stencils and tooling, processes online Gerber quotes, structures multi-facility EMS supply agreements across 30+ North American locations, and highlights StenTech nano-coating advantages. The other track delivers manufacturing precision: it processes CAD Gerber packages in StenCAD, calculates Area Ratios (AR > 0.66), optimizes fine-pitch micro-BGA apertures, generates Fiber Laser G-code, applies BluPrint™ PVD & Advanced Nano™ hydrophobic coatings, designs wave solder pallets, and verifies Solder Paste Inspection (SPI) transfer efficiency. Point one at a Gerber file, an RFQ or a tooling design and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
