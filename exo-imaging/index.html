AI operators that deploy Exo Iris® POCUS handhelds, optimize SweepAI™ real-time vision algorithms, integrate Exo Works® EMR/PACS workflows, and clear FDA 510(k) diagnostics
Independent concept  ·  illustrative
Each AI operator is a clinical commercial or ultrasound engineering workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track captures health system business: it finds emergency departments, ICU networks, primary care clinics and health systems needing point-of-care ultrasound (POCUS), qualifies clinical requirements, structures enterprise health system contracts, audits unbilled ultrasound scans to model +47% revenue capture with Exo Works®, and highlights Exo 3-in-1 pMUT differentiation. The other track delivers product engineering and regulatory excellence: it optimizes pMUT (Piezoelectric Micromachined Ultrasonic Transducer) silicon chip arrays, trains SweepAI™ cardiac (LVEF, EF, strain) and lung models, integrates Exo Works DICOM routers with Epic/Cerner EMRs, and compiles FDA 510(k) clearance packages. Point one at a health system RFP, an ultrasound image dataset or an FDA submission and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
