Each AI operator is a 200G/lane PAM4 Transimpedance Amplifier (TIA), 1.6T EML laser driver, or Linear Pluggable Optics (LPO) analog equalizer engineering workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. Litrinium Inc. (headquartered in Aliso Viejo & Irvine, California) is the high-speed optical and copper interconnect IC pioneer delivering ultra-low power, ultra-high bandwidth signal integrity solutions for massive AI clusters and hyperscale data centers. One track drives commercial sourcing & hyperscale cloud relationships: it captures Hyperscale AI Cluster Architects (NVIDIA, Broadcom, Arista, Meta, Google, Microsoft), Optical Transceiver OEMs (InnoLight, Coherent, Eoptolink, Cisco/Acacia), and Active Copper Cable (ACC) makers, quotes 200G/lane PAM4 TIA & Laser Driver chipset volumes, structures Linear Pluggable Optics (LPO) low-power proposals (< 4W per 800G module), and highlights Litrinium's pure analog design philosophy, lowest power per bit, and TIA bandwidth margin. The other track delivers optical semiconductor engineering precision: it designs 200G/lane PAM4 Transimpedance Amplifiers (TIA bandwidth f_-3dB = 72 GHz, THD < 1.5%), integrates 1.6T EML laser drivers (TDECQ < 1.2 dB, power < 180 mW/ch), tests 800G LPO analog equalizers (channel propagation latency < 5 ps), and audits Aliso Viejo CA 70 GHz+ optical wafer probing lines (S21 Cpk = 1.88). Point one at an optical transceiver spec, a TIA frequency response plot or an LPO equalizer log and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
