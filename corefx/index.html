Each AI operator is a spray-dried lipid powder, CoreNhanced™ bioactive delivery, dry creamer, or clean-label carrier packaging workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track wins the work: it finds sports nutrition brands, functional beverage manufacturers, and CPG private label buyers seeking MCT powders, avocado oil powders, and bioactive nutrients, quotes co-manufacturing supply agreements, responds to global nutrition master tenders, and tracks ingredient market intelligence. The other track delivers powder manufacturing and plant operations: it qualifies 70% MCT oil powder cold-water dispersibility (<8.5 sec, <1.1% surface free fat), optimizes industrial spray dryer yield & 25kg multi-wall bag filling precision (>98.5% recovery, 25.00 kg ±0.02 kg fill), simulates spray dryer chamber CFD thermal kinetics (evaporating 1,800 kg H2O/hr @ 195°C inlet), and manages formula spec ECO revisions. Point one at a sports nutrition customer account, a dispersibility test report, or a spray tower thermal batch log and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a VP of Food R&D or Director of Quality Assurance to approve.
