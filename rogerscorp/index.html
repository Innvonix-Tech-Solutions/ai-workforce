Each AI operator is an advanced electronics or elastomeric materials engineering workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track captures advanced technology business: it finds EV traction inverter OEMs (BorgWarner, Vitesco, Aptiv), 5G telecom base station builders (Ericsson, Nokia), and defense radar contractors requiring RO4000® RF laminates, curamik® ceramic substrates, and PORON®/BISCO® foams, quotes material roll & panel specs, structures AMB qualification proposals, and highlights Rogers Corp market leadership. The other track delivers material engineering precision: it models 77 GHz ADAS radar RF stackups ($D_k / D_f$), designs curamik® Active Metal Brazed (AMB) $Si_3N_4$ power substrates ($R_{th} < 0.15 \text{ K/W}$), simulates PORON® battery cell compression set (<5%), audits ROLINX® busbar partial discharge ($PDIV > 1,500\text{V}$), and signs off IATF/ISO quality travelers. Point one at a PCB stackup drawing, a power module thermal spec or a battery foam test log and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
