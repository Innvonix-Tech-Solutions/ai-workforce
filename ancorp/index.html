AI operators that capture semiconductor OEMs & research labs, design custom UHV vacuum chambers, manufacture #1 patented vacuum ball valves, and certify helium leak rates
Independent concept  ·  illustrative
Each AI operator is a high-vacuum (HV) or ultra-high vacuum (UHV) commercial or manufacturing engineering workflow turned into an autonomous team of agents. One track captures vacuum hardware business: it finds semiconductor tool OEMs (Applied Materials, Lam Research, TEL), thin-film coaters, and national research labs (Oak Ridge, SLAC, Fermilab) requiring CF/ISO flanges, ball valves, and custom vacuum chambers, quotes volume hardware packages, structures custom chamber proposals, and highlights ANCORP's #1 vacuum ball valve patent. The other track delivers vacuum fabrication precision: it models custom 316L stainless steel UHV vessels, sizes ConFlat (CF) & ISO-KF flanges, machines precision valve bodies, runs UHV electropolishing ($R_a < 0.2\,\mu\text{m}$), conducts 100% helium mass spectrometer leak detection ($< 1\times 10^{-10} \text{ std cc/s}$ He), and signs off quality travelers. Point one at a vacuum system RFQ, a chamber CAD file or a helium leak log and it runs the loop, then hands back a review-ready output for a person to approve.
